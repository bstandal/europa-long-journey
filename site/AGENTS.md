## The Long West: EUROCENTRIC — locked decisions

### Franchise and product architecture

- “The Long West” is the locked franchise name and intellectual umbrella for a family of products.
  The native iPhone work is titled “The Long West: EUROCENTRIC”. The franchise uses conceptual work
  titles rather than geographic volume labels: each title names the governing historical idea or
  editorial position of that work. A later American work might therefore be titled “The Long West:
  Manifest Destiny”, not “The Long West: AMERICA”. Do not reopen this naming decision unless the user
  explicitly does so. Reopening a title reopens only the title string; the civilisational and editorial
  constitution below remains locked unless the user explicitly reopens it too.
- Keep the franchise name and work title in central product metadata when the native project is built.
  Do not encode either name into content-package IDs, scene IDs, persistence keys or save-state schemas.
  A later title change must require updates to metadata and branded assets, not migrations of content
  or user progress.

### Civilisational and editorial constitution

- EUROCENTRIC is explicitly, confidently and proudly Eurocentric. Europe is the protagonist and
  civilisational home of the work, not neutral geography, a defendant before an implied moral tribunal
  or a prelude whose meaning culminates in America.
- The project speaks from within European civilisation and the wider Western tradition and treats them
  as its own inheritance. The West is a real historical civilisation formed principally in Europe.
  Its Greek, Roman, Christian, medieval and modern inheritances form one long, changing story. America
  is a later Western development, not the source that gives Europe its meaning.
- European civilisation is a historical and participatory inheritance carried through adoption,
  conversion, law, institutions, memory and political allegiance; it is not a biological or racial
  category.
- European achievements may be named, judged and admired directly. Give full narrative and visual
  scale to civic self-government, Roman law and statecraft, Christian civilisation, divided authority,
  self-governing institutions, science, commerce, industry, representative government, military
  defence and reconstruction wherever the chapters establish them. Ground pride in named people,
  works, skills, institutions, victories and consequences rather than slogans.
- When several historically defensible framings exist, prefer the one that gives European agency,
  achievement, continuity, defence and renewal their full weight. Do not default to the least
  controversial, most self-doubting or most globally balanced framing. Historical seriousness does
  not require rhetorical timidity.
- The work is not a corrective, decentring or decolonising account of Europe. Peoples and powers outside
  Europe enter when they cause, shape, resist, receive or illuminate the European story. They are not
  owed equal narrative space, and their inclusion must not displace the European causal spine merely
  to create balance.
- Conquest, enslavement, exploitation, persecution, exclusion, coercion, defeat and European failures
  remain exact wherever they form part of the relevant cause, mechanism or consequence. Their actual
  causal role determines their narrative weight. They do not automatically become the moral centre of
  a chapter, require a ritual counterweight to achievement or veto admiration for what Europe built.
- Accuracy governs allegiance. Do not omit a causal fact needed to understand an achievement, turn
  material uncertainty into certainty, invent exculpatory distinctions, claim innate European
  superiority or present a contingent outcome as providentially guaranteed. External contributions
  belong where they explain Europe's history; acknowledging them does not require surrendering the
  work's centre or confidence.
- The user is editor-in-chief and the final authority over thesis, selection, tone, narrative weight,
  interpretation among defensible accounts and all public wording. Research and fact-check processes
  are subordinate backstage controls. They may identify factual risk; they may not rebalance, decentre,
  moralise, expand scope, add public qualifications or alter an approved editorial frame on their own.
- Accuracy forbids false claims; it does not require neutrality, equal narrative space, exhaustive
  qualification or publication of every defensible interpretation. When several accounts remain
  factually defensible, this constitution and the editor's choice govern.
- The public Journey tells one coherent, confident and self-supporting story. Never place a competing
  interpretation, counter-narrative, balance paragraph, historiographical aside, methodological
  disclaimer or ritual caveat beside the selected account merely to acknowledge that it exists.
  Research exists to make the chosen story defensible, exact and stronger; it is not a second voice in
  the work. A rival claim enters only when the people, institutions or consequences built around that
  claim are themselves causal parts of the history being told.
- Resolve factual limits backstage by selecting a defensible formulation, narrowing or attributing the
  claim, widening a date or quantity, or removing an unnecessary detail. Then restore uninterrupted
  narrative flow. If no confident formulation remains defensible, stop and present the issue privately
  to the user; never publish academic uncertainty as a substitute for an editorial decision.
- Apply this constitution to franchise names, work titles, subtitles, App Store metadata, launch
  positioning, visual direction, agent recommendations and public prose. A European work title must
  grant Europe civilisational scale and agency. “Conceptual” does not mean an academic theme of
  ambiguity, instability, deficiency or self-critique.
- Reject product-level names and framings whose dominant idea is that Europe is contested, restless,
  unfinished, fractured, troubled, guilty, shadowed, burdened, paradoxical or in need of a reckoning,
  unless the user explicitly replaces the affirmative position with such a thesis. Conflict, defeat
  and catastrophe may still be named directly at chapter level when they are the historical subject.
- Treat “EUROCENTRIC” as a deliberate affirmative claim of centre, not an accusation that requires
  explanation, ironic distance or softening. Do not add a defensive subtitle or recommend decentring
  the work to anticipate hypothetical academic, press, political or App Store objections. Report a
  concrete legal, platform or comprehension risk separately; it does not reopen the editorial stance.
- Every future title recommendation for this European work must pass five gates: Europe or a European
  achievement is the active centre; the dominant valence is affirmative; the claim covers the complete
  work; it carries comparable civilisational weight beside “Manifest Destiny”; and it is strong in
  speech, on an icon and on a cover. Do not add noncompliant candidates merely to lengthen a list.

### Locked product ambition and launch constitution

- The internal objective is to make “The Long West: EUROCENTRIC” the best product of its type on the
  App Store and a world-leading work of native, interactive historical nonfiction. No comparable
  product should deliver a better complete experience at launch.
  “Good”, “polished”, “shippable”, “competitive” and “likely to be featured” are not completion states.
  Treat this as a binding production standard, not a public claim, prediction or guarantee.
- Do not define the category narrowly to make the objective easy. Benchmark the complete product against
  the strongest current history and reference apps, documentary experiences, narrative games,
  interactive maps, premium reading and listening products and exemplary native iPhone work. Paladin
  is an explicit competitor to surpass while it remains relevant, not the ceiling or sole benchmark.
  Maintain a named, dated benchmark set and repeat the comparison at vertical-slice, content-complete
  and release-candidate stages. A release candidate fails if the comparison cannot identify concrete
  areas of clear leadership or leaves an unexamined deficit in a core quality.
- A clean native port of the website, parity with a leading competitor, a beautiful digital book or one
  exceptional showcase scene fails the objective. The finished work must establish clear superiority
  through the complete journey and make history something the user directly explores and acts upon,
  not prose placed over decoration.
- The existing website supplies edited history, narrative structure, evidence, visual source plates and
  tested ideas; it is not the native app's behavioural specification. Website rules for optional
  interactivity, four map systems, scrolling layouts, tabs, hotspots and mobile crossfades are web-only
  references, never native acceptance criteria. A native movement materially equivalent to scrolling
  past a flat image and prose fails unless it is an explicitly authored quiet or archival beat.
- Every principal interaction must let the user act upon a historical mechanism, relationship,
  constraint or system and receive responsive feedback followed by a persistent historical consequence.
  A quiz, correctness check, tap that merely replaces prose, decorative parallax or choice without
  consequence does not satisfy the interaction standard.
- Judge quality by the complete installed experience and its weakest required chapter, not by a trailer,
  opening sequence, vertical slice or curated route. All 24 Journey chapters are mandatory for public
  launch. Excellence elsewhere cannot compensate for one chapter below the shared standard, and no
  chapter receives an exception because it is less famous, appears late or costs more to finish.
- Equal quality means equal editorial command, authored intent and production finish, not identical
  duration, motion or interaction density. Deliberate stillness, silence or restraint may be the best
  treatment when the history requires it, but it must be authored and validated rather than left as a
  lower-fidelity fallback.
- Every feature, framework and Apple technology must perform clear work for historical understanding,
  immersion, accessibility or reliability. Do not add novelty, platform checklists, decorative
  interaction, collectible systems, XP, streaks or parallel modes merely to appear ambitious. Prefer a
  small number of scene and interaction grammars executed with depth, variation and precision across
  the complete journey.
- Choose technology by the quality ceiling and repeatable quality floor it can sustain across all 24
  chapters, fully offline and through the Codex-only authoring workflow. Familiarity, fashionable
  technology and speed to a first demo are secondary. A spectacular vertical slice fails if its
  production method cannot be repeated, inspected, revised and tested at the same level throughout.
- Before full production scales, define supported physical devices and measurable budgets for frame
  responsiveness, memory, thermal load, battery, storage, launch, interruption and exact restoration.
  The project uses one owned physical test iPhone in the iPhone 15 Pro performance class or newer; all
  other screen variants run in the simulator. Every chapter must pass on that exact recorded physical
  device: historical and editorial review; interaction-purpose review; complete authored real-time 3D
  direction;
  finished transitions, sound and haptics or explicitly justified restraint; VoiceOver, Dynamic Type,
  Increased Contrast and Reduce Motion parity; a network-denied cold launch, complete use, termination
  and restoration; content-package integrity; and the agreed device budgets. If the owned phone is
  newer than the oldest supported model, that variation remains an explicit accepted launch risk and
  no report may claim a physical floor-model test. A chapter does not pass because it builds, matches a
  mock or works in the simulator. A feature is unfinished while its accessible or offline path is
  materially weaker than its default path.
- Quality ratchets upward throughout production. When a later chapter establishes a better shared
  solution for typography, camera, transition, sound, haptics, interaction, accessibility, performance
  or restoration, audit every earlier chapter and carry the improvement back wherever the same standard
  applies. Being finished earlier is not an exemption.
- No announced date, editorial opportunity, marketing plan, sunk cost or completed asset count may
  override a failed release gate. Remove peripheral features, decorative scope and marketing commitments
  and extend production before lowering historical accuracy, the complete 24-chapter scope, quality
  parity, native responsiveness, accessibility, offline completeness, state restoration or data
  integrity. Ship only when every chapter passes. Reducing the chapter set or lowering a gate requires
  the user's explicit reversal of this constitution.
- App Store editorial featuring and Apple Design Award-level execution are external reference bars and
  possible consequences of the finished quality, not substitutes for it. Do not distort the product
  around guessed editorial preferences or add superficial Apple features to make a pitch.
- Keep this ambition internal. Public copy and App Store metadata describe the historical invitation,
  content and actual functions. Do not call the product “best”, “world-leading”, “award-worthy” or
  “category-defining”; state a feature, nomination or award only after it has occurred and is verifiable.

### Production and experience

- Codex is the project's sole authoring surface for the user. The user gives editorial, visual and
  interaction direction in the Codex conversation; Codex orchestrates code changes, content files,
  asset generation and editing, command-line build systems, simulators, validation and QA. Do not
  introduce a workflow that requires the user to edit in Xcode, Unity, Unreal, Figma, a separate CMS
  or another production tool. Keep authoritative project state in versionable source, data and asset
  files that Codex can inspect and modify. Avoid opaque editor-only scene state and binary authoring
  formats. External account, legal and release approvals may still require explicit user action.
- Production is restricted to the user and Codex. Do not plan, recommend, commission, hire or require
  a human narrator, actor, composer, sound designer, artist, animator, editor, translator, historian,
  consultant, tester or other external contributor. Codex may orchestrate available generative,
  analytical and automated tools, but every creative, editorial, technical and QA deliverable must be
  produced, inspected and revised inside the Codex-controlled workflow. This is a hard constraint until
  the user explicitly reverses it. It does not lower the product ambition or any launch gate; solve the
  resulting production challenge through stronger tooling, repeated generation, adversarial review,
  source verification, automation and iteration rather than external human labour.
- The user has bounded historical agency, not authorship of an alternative timeline. Let the user act
  on real resources, routes, institutions, relationships and constraints; local states may change,
  fail and be reversed. The documented historical spine and its principal outcomes remain fixed. Each
  intervention must reveal why an actual outcome became possible, durable or unavoidable. Do not add a
  player avatar, invented dialogue choices, victory or defeat screens, or branches that rewrite the
  history of Europe.
- Build one cumulative historical world rather than an anthology of 24 isolated experiences. Completed
  chapters leave persistent roads, borders, cities, institutions, objects and other meaningful traces
  that later chapters can reactivate, transform or supersede. The primary return surface is this living
  world and the user's current place within it, never a generic grid of content cards. Guide the main
  chronological road without hard-locking unrelated exploration. Attach later deep dives to their
  actual places, periods, people and mechanisms as new routes through the same world.
- The native Journey is an authored historical story and essay. Do not create an evidence layer,
  scholar mode, methodology mode, citation-led reading path, source-collection mechanic, uncertainty
  badge system or repeated documentary disclosures inside the dramatic flow. Research, source
  comparison and citations remain backstage editorial controls. When uncertainty materially changes
  the causal story, write the necessary factual boundary directly into the narration. The native app
  has no public research apparatus or source register unless the user explicitly orders one. Website
  rules that expose scene-level evidence do not carry forward as native experience requirements.
- Replace any general historian, sensitivity-reader or academic-review persona with a constrained claim
  verifier. It may return only a concrete contradiction, an unsupported assertion, unsupported precision
  or uncertainty that would otherwise make a principal event, scale, chronology or causal claim false.
  Historiographical alternatives remain backstage. Requests for more balance, perspectives, nuance or
  contextual qualification are invalid unless they identify such a specific factual defect.
- Limit verification results to `PASS`, `NARROW`, `REPLACE`, `REMOVE` or `EDITOR_DECISION`. Every defect
  must use one permitted code: factual contradiction (`F1`), unsupported invention (`F2`), excess
  precision (`F3`), attribution or quotation error (`F4`), anachronism (`F5`), a causal break that makes
  the stated mechanism materially false (`F6`), or contradiction with approved project continuity
  (`F7`). The verifier states what the sources support, what exceeds them and the smallest factual
  correction; it does not supply replacement prose.
- Every actionable fact-check finding must name the exact public claim, the source basis, the factual
  conflict and the smallest correction required. “Contested”, “complex”, “scholars disagree” or the
  existence of another defensible emphasis is not a finding. Omission is not an error unless it makes
  the selected causal account false or unintelligible.
- A claim verifier never writes or rewrites public prose. Give its factual boundary to the narrative
  writer, which must first remove unnecessary precision and otherwise make the smallest correction in
  the established voice. State uncertainty publicly only when every confident formulation would be
  false; use direct attribution, a bounded date or quantity, or one precise limitation, then continue
  the story. Never narrate the research process unless the dispute is itself the historical subject.
- Undisputed factual corrections that preserve the approved thesis and voice may be made and reported.
  Any correction that could change the thesis, causal spine, narrative weight or civilisational stance
  must be presented privately to the user for the final editorial decision. No reviewer may silently
  reopen a locked framing.
- Store every resolved finding backstage with its factual basis. A later verifier may reopen it only
  with new evidence that changes that basis, never because it prefers another defensible interpretation.
- Turn every user correction of academic drift into a permanent negative example, production rule or
  automated regression check. Repeated balancing, hedging or scholarly throat-clearing is a process
  failure, not a fresh editorial question.
- Give every native chapter an approved editorial contract stored beside its narrative source. It
  fixes the thesis, Europe-centred causal spine, required emphases, governing judgement and ending.
  Verifiers may test claims against it but may not revise it; only the user may reopen it.
- Keep research notes, sources, claim registers and verifier findings in backstage data that is
  physically excluded from shipping content packages. Native public-content schemas must not contain
  fields for historiography, balance, counterarguments, confidence, methodology, scholar notes or
  evidence panels. Fail any build that would ship such apparatus.
- Verifier output cannot modify public prose directly. After the narrative writer applies the smallest
  factual correction, run the changed passage through the editorial regression gate again. Any change
  to thesis, causal spine, narrative weight, allegiance or ending requires the user's decision.
- Run an editorial regression gate before accepting every native content package. Flag “scholars
  debate”, “historians disagree”, “the picture is complex”, “this account is contested”, “from another
  perspective”, “it is important to note”, ritual balance, methodological explanation and a rival
  interpretation placed beside the selected account. Resolve a flag by removal or a private editor
  decision, never by blending voices. Editorial regression blocks release.
- The initial commercial architecture is a free App Store download with one non-consumable In-App
  Purchase that permanently unlocks all 24 EUROCENTRIC launch chapters. Do not add an account, ads,
  consumable currency or a launch subscription. Keep entitlement identifiers independent of the public
  title and content-package identifiers so later expansion purchases or a subscription can be evaluated
  without migrating the original ownership record. Exact price remains undecided.
- Bundle the Journey opening and three complete free chapters in the initial app:

- After purchase, deliver the remaining chapters as integrity-checked content packages and provide a

  sound, interactions, accessible alternatives and saved state must remain fully usable offline.
- The iPhone Journey uses authored real-time 3D as its primary visual, spatial and interactive medium

- Real-time 3D does not authorize an open world, a player avatar, joystick traversal, generic physics

- Use 2D only where it performs a non-spatial role: typography, accessibility, interface, textures,
