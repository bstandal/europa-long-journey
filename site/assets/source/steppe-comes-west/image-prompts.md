# Steppe Comes West — production prompts

## Shared direction

All landscape scenes were generated at 1536×1024. Mobile compositions were generated independently
at 1024×1536. The approved Farmers image
`/Users/bard/.codex/generated_images/019f70e6-72f7-7271-b3a9-f2c0d7433470/call_p0aJgn4PD9ITemcQwtC7csP2.png`
was supplied as a style reference for restrained cinematic realism, natural texture, deep tonal range
and museum-grade seriousness. It was not used as a compositional template.

Historical exclusions applied throughout: no cavalry before the late third millennium BC; no saddles,
stirrups, armour, swords, spoke-wheeled chariots or later Central Asian tents in early scenes; no
fantasy, invented script or supernatural effects.

## 01 — Where the Fields End

> c. 3300 BC, Lower Dnipro, blue-green dawn. Seen from the settled European side, the last cultivated
> plots and dark woodland in the left foreground open into an immense low grassland horizon. A narrow
> river glints through the transition. Two or three distant farming figures stand near a rough boundary
> path while a small pastoral group with cattle appears far beyond. Compose with generous dark negative
> space on the lower-left for editorial typography. Keep the circle motif latent in a curved river bend
> and loose arc of cattle.

Mobile:

> Recompose as a vertical 2:3 poster, not a crop. Put dark cultivated soil, a short wattle fence and two
> small farmer silhouettes in the lower third; let a curving river open into steppe and place a distant
> arc of cattle beneath the low dawn horizon. Preserve central and lower-left text-safe space.

## 02 — A World That Could Move

> 3300–3000 BC, Pontic-Caspian Steppe, pale morning. A wide mobile pastoral camp occupies the middle
> distance beneath an enormous sky. Cattle, sheep, hide-covered shelters, goods, women, men and children
> form a loose, practical circle around work and a low fire. Show seasonal occupation and a distant
> watercourse. Keep the camp at center-right and darker text-safe space at left.

## 03 — The Wheel Carries the House

> 3300–2800 BC, Dnipro to Lower Danube. Use an extremely low camera beside the massive solid wooden disc
> wheel of an ox-drawn wagon. The wheel fills the center-right foreground. The heavy plank wagon carries
> shelter material, vessels, food stores and household goods; adults and children walk beside it with
> cattle and sheep. Show weight and practical reach, not speed. Preserve open negative space at left.

## 04 — The Mound Holds the Fathers

> 3200–2600 BC, steppe to Carpathian Basin. Use a high oblique, nearly overhead view of a newly raised
> low kurgan in long late-afternoon light. The earth circle sits center-right around a partly visible
> grave cut and timber or stone setting. Armed adult men stand on foot around the edge with simple axes,
> bows and spears at rest; families remain farther back. Long shadows radiate from the mound. Leave the
> left side quiet and dark.

## 05 — The Fathers Disappear

> 2900–2000 BC, central European farming landscape at cold dusk. Show one direct confrontation: four or
> five incoming armed men on foot face three local farming men near a fence and timber house while
> families withdraw toward a dark doorway. A trampled field and broken fence make control of land and
> household legible. No charge, bodies, gore or theatrical battle pose. Keep both groups center-right
> and text-safe space at left.

Mobile:

> Recompose as a deep vertical corridor, not a crop. Two or three incoming men approach from the
> upper-middle; two local men stand lower-right beside a broken fence and house, with family members in
> the doorway. Keep both sides within the central phone-width band and a quiet dark lower-middle.

## 06 — Speech Follows Power

> 2900–2000 BC, Corded Ware Europe at night. An intimate household gathering forms a warm circle around
> a low fire at center-right. An older speaker addresses children and younger adults; a woman from
> another household sits among them and two armed men stand quietly beyond the light. Show oral
> transmission through family, marriage and power. Preserve broad dark space on the left.

## 07 — The Gods Take Two Roads

> After c. 2500 BC. A dawn ritual scene seen low and wide. A circular fire ring at center-right has burned
> to coals. Smoke catches first light and aligns with a large pale sun disc on the horizon. Two small
> groups take diverging paths, one west into darker woodland and one east across grassland. Suggest
> Eurasian routes through the landscape without drawing a literal map. Keep deep text-safe space at left.

Mobile:

> Recompose vertically, not as a crop. Place the fire circle in the lower third, a narrow smoke column
> through the centre and the pale sun in the upper third. At mid-height two groups depart left toward
> woodland and right across open grassland. Keep the material-to-mythic circle progression natural.

## Bronze Europe handoff

> Later third millennium BC, predawn on a dark ridge. A large sun disc rises through smoke with a dull
> bronze gleam. In the foreground-right, a craftsperson lifts a newly cast plain bronze axe head near a
> low charcoal fire. A low mound and one partly buried solid wooden disc wheel remain barely visible; a
> distant waterway catches the first metallic light. Keep the left two-thirds dark for monumental ending
> typography. No wagon silhouette or spoke wheel.

The first Bronze handoff output was rejected because it introduced a later spoke-wheeled wagon. Only
the corrected solid-disc version is preserved in the project.
