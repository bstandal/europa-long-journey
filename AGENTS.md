## Editorial authority and fact-check firewall

- The user is the final authority over thesis, selection, tone, narrative weight, interpretation among
  defensible accounts and all public wording. Research and fact-check processes are subordinate
  backstage controls. They may identify factual risk; they may not rebalance, decentre, moralise,
  expand scope, add public qualifications or alter an approved editorial frame on their own.
- Fact-check concrete public claims, not editorial allegiance. An actionable objection must identify
  the exact claim, the specific falsehood, unsupported precision or causally material omission, its
  source basis and the smallest factual correction required. “Contested”, “complex”, “scholars
  disagree”, a missing perspective or another defensible emphasis is not by itself a factual defect.
- Accuracy forbids false claims. It does not require neutrality, equal narrative space, exhaustive
  qualification or publication of every defensible interpretation. When several accounts remain
  factually defensible, the project constitution and the editor's choice govern.
- The public Journey tells one coherent, confident and self-supporting story. Never place a competing
  interpretation, counter-narrative, balance paragraph, historiographical aside, methodological
  disclaimer or ritual caveat beside the selected account merely to acknowledge that it exists.
  Research exists to make the chosen story defensible, exact and stronger; it is not a second voice in
  the work. A rival claim enters only when the people, institutions or consequences built around that
  claim are themselves causal parts of the history being told.
- Resolve factual limits backstage by selecting a defensible formulation, narrowing or attributing the
  claim, widening a date or quantity, or removing an unnecessary detail. Then restore uninterrupted
  narrative flow. If no confident formulation remains defensible, stop and present the issue privately
  to the user; never publish academic uncertainty as a substitute for an editorial decision.
- Resolve uncertainty backstage. Put it into the Journey only when every unqualified formulation would
  make a principal event, scale, chronology or causal claim false. Use direct attribution, a bounded
  date or quantity, or one precise limitation, then continue the story. Never narrate historiography or
  the research process unless that dispute is itself the historical subject.
- Claim registers, sources, confidence scores, counterarguments and source disputes cannot generate
  public caveats, badges, panels, `What survived`, evidence views or optional modes. The frozen iPhone
  product had no public research apparatus. That historical choice does not decide how the future Mac
  product presents the website's existing public sources or evidence.
- A verifier never writes public prose. It returns a structured factual finding; the narrative writer
  makes the smallest necessary correction in the established voice. A correction that could alter the
  thesis, causal spine, narrative weight or civilisational stance must be presented privately to the
  user for the final editorial decision.
- A verification result is limited to `PASS`, `NARROW`, `REPLACE`, `REMOVE` or `EDITOR_DECISION`.
  A defect must be one of: factual contradiction (`F1`), unsupported invention (`F2`), excess precision
  (`F3`), attribution or quotation error (`F4`), anachronism (`F5`), a causal break that makes the stated
  mechanism materially false (`F6`), or contradiction with approved project continuity (`F7`). No
  general request for nuance, balance, context or another perspective is a valid defect.
- A closed verification issue cannot be reopened without new evidence that changes its factual basis.
  Store the resolution backstage so another agent cannot restart a settled editorial debate merely by
  preferring a different interpretation.
- Every user correction of academic drift becomes a permanent negative example, rule or automated
  regression check. Do not allow the same balancing, hedging or scholarly-throat-clearing pattern to
  return in later chapters or production stages.

## Frozen iPhone editorial enforcement — historical

The rules in this section are retained for the archived iPhone production. They do not specify the
Mac product or its architecture. The project-wide editorial authority above remains active.

- Give every native chapter an approved editorial contract stored beside its narrative source. The
  contract fixes the chapter thesis, Europe-centred causal spine, required emphases, governing
  judgement and ending. Research and verification may test factual claims against it; they may not
  revise the contract. Only the user may reopen it.
- Keep research notes, claim registers, sources and verifier findings in backstage project data that
  is physically excluded from shipping content packages. Native public-content schemas must not have
  fields for historiography, balance, counterarguments, confidence scores, methodology, scholar notes
  or evidence panels. A build that would ship any such apparatus must fail.
- Verifier output cannot modify public prose directly. It produces only a structured `F1`–`F7`
  finding. The narrative writer applies the smallest factual repair in the approved voice, then the
  changed passage passes the editorial regression gate again. Any change to thesis, causal spine,
  narrative weight, allegiance or ending stops for the user's decision.
- Run an editorial regression gate before every native content package is accepted. It must flag
  academic leakage such as “scholars debate”, “historians disagree”, “the picture is complex”, “this
  account is contested”, “from another perspective”, “it is important to note”, ritual balance,
  methodological explanation and rival interpretations placed beside the selected account. A flag is
  resolved by removal or a private editor decision, never by blending voices.
- Every writer, reviewer, subagent and automated production tool must receive this constitution and
  the affected chapter contract. Noncompliant output is rejected rather than averaged into approved
  prose. Editorial regression is a release-blocking failure on the same level as corrupt content,
  broken restoration or failed offline use.

## Frozen iPhone launch decisions — historical

The decisions below document the attempted iPhone product. They are inactive and must not be carried
into Mac exploration without a new, explicit user decision.

- Launch in English only, with a localisation-ready content and interface architecture. Do not let
  future localisation requirements weaken or genericise the approved English voice.
- Launch on iPhone in portrait orientation only. Compose every scene, interaction, caption and
  accessible alternative for the portrait canvas; landscape is outside launch scope.
- Bundle the Journey opening and the three complete free chapters in the initial app: `first-farmers`
  (Chapter 01, “The First Farmers”), `europe-holds-the-line` (Chapter 13, “The Frontiers Hold”) and
  `european-world` (Chapter 21, “The European World”). These chapters provide deliberately different
  historical and experiential tastes. The free selection is keyed by stable content IDs, never by
  ordinal chapter numbers.
- One non-consumable In-App Purchase permanently unlocks all 24 EUROCENTRIC launch chapters. Keep the
  three free chapters in the base install; offer the remaining chapters as integrity-checked content
  packages after purchase, including a clear `Download all` action. Once a package is installed, its
  complete experience and saved state work without a network connection. Purchase, restore, push and
  initial content delivery are the only permitted network edges for the launch product.
- Provide an optional, pre-produced English narration track that follows user-controlled pacing,
  restores the exact committed source cursor after controlled pause or backgrounding and remains fully
  available offline after installation. Sudden process death resumes paused from the latest durable
  cursor within the physically proven bound; this is not a promise of sample-perfect loudspeaker
  output. Narration is distinct from accessibility VoiceOver and is never generated live at runtime.
  Narration, score, soundscape, spatial detail, silence and haptics are one authored dramatic system
  and must reach the same exceptional artistic bar as image, prose and interaction.
- Preserve the dark identity through the rule `Darkness frames the evidence`: near-black frames and
  separates; readable midtones reveal people, materials and action; one historically meaningful
  mechanism carries the clearest light. Do not brighten the product into a generic interface or let
  uniform underexposure hide the period-specific world.
