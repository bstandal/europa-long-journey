minutes. Retire the current 28-minute sequence of 17 visible beats as a public

## 2. The playable premise

> Carry the living farming system onto European ground, then make it survive
> the season, the household and the generation.

The user is a working presence without an avatar or invented identity. At each
historical threshold, touch enters the labour: pull, hold, carry, divide,
raise, replace and open. The historical outcome is fixed. Local material state
can resist, remain incomplete and be revised until the obligation is met.

Seed grain is the recurring causal carrier. It travels with households, enters
the furrow, returns as a finite harvest, remains uneaten through winter and
moves with later households. It is a repeated mechanism, not one symbolic or
magical seed. The scale grows around it:

`grain → store → house → clearing → settlement belt → continent`

Every principal action is shared with visible people. One recognisable young
member of the first household helps protect the seed, repair the winter loss
and return grain to earth. Later people repeat the learned handling gesture
without pretending that one face, family or vessel crosses centuries. The
chapter's recurring sound is physical grain moving in real containers: it is
masked by water, disappears while sealed and returns on spring soil.

## 3. Five 3D world cells, one journey

The production unit is five bounded, streamable 3D world cells rather than 17
illustrated scenes:

1. Western Anatolian shore and Aegean passage
2. Thessalian field, household and store
3. Iron Gates riverbank
4. Danube and loess longhouse ground
5. Expanding settlement landscape and continental relief

The Iron Gates cell establishes its older river world before farming cargo
touches the bank, then holds one fixed landing through a brief passage of time.
At most the current and next cell need to be live. Streaming is hidden by a
causal transition and never exposed as navigation.

## 5. Transition grammar

Every change of place, time or scale follows the state the user just created.
At least one physical carrier survives each transition, and no prop changes
identity merely to hide a cut.

1. The actual seed vessel leaves the loaded boat and reaches Thessalian soil in
   the household's hands.
2. The camera remains with the furrow as crop rises, is cut and becomes the
   finite grain beneath the store roof.
3. Spring seed leaves the sealed vessel, enters a travel sack and returns to a
   real boat moving north.
4. The established Iron Gates bank is legible before the arriving cargo moves
   in either direction.
5. An actual household, seed sack and tools continue from the landing, up the
   worn route and are set beside the prepared longhouse ground. A worker then
   gives the user the real lifting line; no carried load changes identity.
6. The longhouse threshold path carries a household and seed into the daughter
   clearing.
7. Visible handoffs carry different households and newly made baskets along the
   accumulated route. At the final local threshold, one loaded barrier holds
   herd, worked soil, water, household and dry store in a single action before
   the camera rises from persistent ground to continental scale.
8. Wind through settled grain continues into the grass beyond the eastern
   horizon for the Chapter 02 handoff.

No cut may use a card, black screen, progress message or camera flourish as a
substitute for historical continuity. Brief darkness is allowed only when it
belongs to the physical scene, such as entering a store or passing beneath a
roof.

## 6. Time grammar

- Time advances only after the user's action has created a stable historical
  state.
- A time jump remains centred on something that survives it: the landing
  stone, sealed seed, posthole, threshold, path or field edge.
- Weather, work, wear, growth and sound announce passing time before a date
  does.
- Physics may animate response, but deterministic domain state decides every
  accepted action and consequence.
- There is no generic time control. Iron Gates first completes a concrete
  two-way exchange; only then may time pass around the fixed landing stone.

## 7. Interaction and usability

The entire chapter uses one tactile vocabulary: `contact`, `grip`, `drag`,
`resistance`, `transfer` and `seal`. The object moves or answers immediately
on contact. Historical affordances come from material tension, light, sound,
body attention and haptics rather than glowing hotspots or instructional
overlays.

The six approved principal interactions keep their stable IDs and domain
grammars, but lose their generic control metaphors:

- the route is a voyage through 3D space, not a line drawn across a flat map;
- harvest is continuous visible grain with quantised authority backstage, not
  12 displayed shares;
- contact is a concrete two-way exchange at one riverbank, not a time slider;
- the longhouse is raised through situated work, not a four-piece puzzle tray;
- settlement growth comes from seed, hearth, field edge and herd lane, not a
  population slider;
- continental change places the complete local system under one changing
  barrier load while surrounding households carry repeated work; it is not a
  three-station recap, map reveal or passive camera-only interaction.

The stable haptic patterns remain attached to their interaction contracts:
crossing `contact/drag/seal`; allocation
`contact/resistance/transfer/seal`; Iron Gates and the two settlement
transforms `drag/break/seal`; house load `contact/resistance/seal`. They report
material events and never form a separate reward layer.

There is no fail screen. An unmet obligation has a legible material result and
remains reversible until the historical state is deliberately sealed. If the
user hesitates, the world first redirects attention through sound, gaze,
movement or light. Only then may a one-to-four-word action cue appear.

Active play has no permanent chapter chrome. One tap outside an active gesture
reveals a compact pause, narration and accessibility surface, which withdraws
without moving the world. Exact restoration removes the need for `Return to
current`.

VoiceOver and direct touch submit the same domain actions and reach the same
world-effect hash. Reduce Motion uses discrete authored state changes while
preserving material order, timing control and consequence. Dynamic Type text
stays outside the rendered 3D action region.

## 8. Language and narration budget

The current review projection contains 1,334 narrated words. The action-pass
flow manuscript keeps 10 short provisional cues at 192 words and under two
minutes at a natural historical pace. The hard greybox ceiling remains 280
words. Captions appear as timed phrases of no more than two lines, never as a
reading panel.

- The first narration follows the stabilised loaded hull; it does not explain
  the scene before the user acts.
- A cue names the meaning of something the user has already experienced.
- A cue lasts no more than about 15 seconds and does not compete with precise
  manipulation.
- Default play has no body copy. Place and date appear briefly in composed
  darkness. Total visible location, instruction and transition language should
  remain below 70 words across the first playthrough.
- With narration disabled, the same short cues appear as no more than two text
  lines in a region that does not cover the action-bearing material.
- Longer website passages remain editorial input. Georgian wine, qvevri,
  genome-wide cognitive-performance variants, pigmentation excursus and the
  language-tree model do not enter the playable causal spine already approved
  for Chapter 01.

Public wording is written only after the silent 3D journey works. The final
line remains the approved ending:

> By 3300 BC, Europe had become a continent of fields, herds and long-lived
> settlements.

## 9. Silent comprehension gate

Before final narration is written, a complete greybox run with narration and
captions disabled must make these relations visible:

1. Europe was already inhabited.
2. Farming arrived with people carrying a complete living system.
3. One harvest had to answer present need, loss and the next spring.
4. Farming and river life met in actual communities.
5. Stores, houses and worked ground bound later generations to place.
6. Growing households demanded more land, while some settlements contracted.
7. The steppe would meet a continent already fixed by fields, herds and
   inherited ground.

If a relation fails this gate, revise action, staging or consequence before
adding explanation.

## 10. Visual and performance scope

- All action-bearing terrain, water edges, tools, grain, stores, structures,
  people, animals and persistent traces use genuine 3D geometry and state.
- The camera remains low, close to work and fully authored. There is no free
  camera or locomotion system.
- People are framed at working distance. Weight, hands, posture, clothing and
  relation to material carry human presence. Chapter 01 does not require
  dialogue scenes, lip sync, close facial acting, free cloth or hair
  simulation, or autonomous crowd AI.
- The selected Harvest images remain composition and lighting references for
  the Thessalian cell. None of their pixels, masks or inferred depth become
  shipping scene state.
- `Darkness frames the evidence`: readable midtones reveal work and settlement;
  the mechanism under action receives the clearest light. Atmosphere may add
  depth but cannot obscure the historical object.
- The engine is not part of this experience approval. The recommended native
  candidate is tested before commitment; targeted custom rendering is added
  only where it materially raises water, grain, soil or time transformation.

The existing physical-device budgets remain the initial gate: 60 fps target,
p99 displayed frame time at or below 25 ms, visible response within 50 ms,
sustained physical memory at or below 500 MiB, thermal state nominal or fair,
restored frame within 1.5 seconds and full interaction within 2 seconds. The
free-package storage ceiling must be remeasured from the finished quality cell
rather than assumed from the 2.5D plan.

## 11. Production proof sequence

No final chapter asset is produced before the full greybox journey works.

1. **3D substrate proof** — one simple cell with authored camera, direct touch,
   VoiceOver, deterministic state, exact restoration and offline package load.
2. **Figure and material proof** — a working household, child, cattle and
   small livestock with wet rope, water, earth and grain at the actual portrait
   camera distance.
3. **80-second continuity proof** — loaded boat → shared stabilisation → dry
   seed vessel → Thessalian ground, with first action inside two seconds, no
   prop identity change and no reading panel or visible load boundary.
4. **190-second truth slice** — finite harvest → allocation → sealed seed →
   winter breach → shared repair → spring sowing, including the grain motif,
   silence, haptics, accessibility and a hard termination/restoration test.
5. **Complete greybox** — the entire 16–18-minute journey, all causal
   transitions and a provisional audio timeline, understandable without prose.
6. **One finished quality cell** — complete Thessalian cell at final art,
   animation, lighting, sound and performance quality. Its measured asset and
   revision method must be repeatable through the other four cells.
7. **Full Chapter 01 production** — remaining cells, final narration, complete
   score and soundscape, accessibility variants, offline package and device
   gate.

The 80-second continuity proof tests whether 3D has solved the experience
problem. The truth slice tests whether the central historical mechanism feels
like play and whether earlier choice returns as action. The finished Thessalian
cell tests whether the quality can be repeated. A beautiful still or isolated
interaction cannot substitute for any of those proofs.

## 12. Acceptance gates for the chapter

- first meaningful physical action available within two seconds;
- no forced passive interval longer than 30 seconds;
- no narration cue longer than about 15 seconds;
- no principal interaction remains materially unchanged for more than about
  45 seconds;
- every place, time and scale transition follows a physical causal carrier and
  no prop changes identity to disguise a cut;
- no body-copy panel appears during active play;
- all six approved interactions leave their approved persistent trace;
- standard touch, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion produce equivalent historical
  consequence;
- termination at every transition and mid-interaction restores the exact
  causal point without replay or a recovery button;
- the complete installed chapter passes offline, integrity, physical-device,
  editorial and silent-comprehension gates;
- the final editor review judges the whole 16–18-minute run, not a curated
  route or hero scene.
