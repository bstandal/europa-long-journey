## 1. The promise

The chapter begins in motion and does not settle into a sequence of historical
illustrations. The user enters a chain of physical obligations:

`keep it dry → make it grow → divide it → repair the loss → reach the bank → hold the load → make room → carry it farther`

Each action buys only a few seconds of relief. Its result creates the next
pressure before the experience can reset. The unresolved question is:

> Can a household keep a fragile living system intact long enough for it to
> become the ground beneath a continent?

The historical answer is fixed. The user does not invent an outcome. The user
feels why the outcome required repeated human work under water, season, loss,
load, decay and finite land.

The authored core is `13:35`. A natural first play is expected to take about
`16:00–17:30` because touch, reading of material states and short observation
remain user-paced. Nothing waits merely to extend duration.

## 2. What action means here

Chapter 01 has no battle, ruler or documented individual to carry suspense.
Its action comes from dependence under physical pressure:

- something real is already moving, slipping, wetting, emptying or failing;
- another person is visibly doing their part and depends on the user's part;
- the user's input changes weight, tension, volume, access or direction at
  once;
- commitment leaves a material result that cannot be cosmetically reset;
- that result is tested within the next two minutes;
- the test creates the next action before relief becomes a stopping page.

There are no quick-time prompts, secret clocks, fail screens, fabricated
disasters or heroic rescues. Releasing a load returns it to a safe incomplete
state. A missed obligation stays open and legible. Intensity comes from
overlapping work and consequence, not punishment.

## 3. Compulsion rules

- First meaningful contact is available within two seconds.
- The user encounters the material problem before narration names it.
- No passive camera passage lasts more than about eight seconds.
- A material state changes roughly every 15–25 seconds.
- A new pressure, reversal, discovery or payoff lands roughly every 45–70
  seconds.
- Relief lasts no longer than about ten seconds before the next obligation is
  visible or audible.
- No principal action is performed alone. Other people brace, receive, carry,
  lift, guide, repair or continue the work.
- Each interaction changes the historical question. No disguised repetition
  of sowing, dragging or placement survives the cut.
- Time advances only from a stable state made by accepted action.
- Transitions follow a real person, load, route, surface, sound or trace.
- Narration never competes with precise manipulation.
- If an interval needs narration, spectacle or interface chrome to remain
  interesting, rebuild or cut the interval.

## 4. Human and sensory spine

One recognisable young member of the first household, old enough to work beside
adults, shares the Aegean crossing, Thessalian season, winter repair and spring
sowing. The person has no name, dialogue, invented biography or special gaze
towards the user. Recognition comes from face, build, clothing and a repeated
two-handed way of steadying the seed vessel.

The young person does not become a hunger meter or a victim staged to
manipulate emotion. They protect the far side of the vessel while the user
holds the near load, help lift the wet food container in winter and place seed
back into earth in spring. Their continuing work makes the deferred future
human.

Later periods use different people, newly made containers and changed clothes.
The two-handed support gesture recurs as learned practice, never as an
implausibly immortal face, object or bloodline.

Seed has one physical audio-haptic motif:

- dry grain shifting inside ceramic gives an irregular close granular sound;
- danger changes that sound through the vessel's real tilt while water masks
  it;
- sealing spring seed removes it completely from the soundscape;
- opening the vessel in spring returns it before the lid is fully clear;
- grain touching soil softens the same material rhythm;
- later vessels have different resonances while preserving the recognisable
  pattern of handling.

It is never a musical jingle. Its winter absence and spring return provide the
first major emotional payoff.

The curve does not alternate between seven self-contained episodes. It is one
movement. Spring seed is already travelling when the first household releases
it. The Iron Gates route is already pulling inland when the exchange ends. The
house is already crowded when its post settles. A later household is already
crossing the empty clearing when the local contraction becomes clear.

## 6. Timecoded experience screenplay

### Sequence 01 — Keep the future alive

**Authored time:** `0:00–2:30`

**Place:** Western Anatolia, the Aegean and Thessaly, 7000–6000 BC

**Principal interaction:**
`interaction-first-farmers-a-household-crosses` · `trace`

**Historical obligation:** Make migration by households and the completeness
of the farming system physically inseparable, then turn carried seed into one
finite harvest before the user can regard arrival as success.

#### `0:00–0:05` — Contact before context

Near-black water presses across the portrait frame at hand height. A loaded
hull has already entered a cross-current. An animal shifts. The hull leans.
Spray reaches the outer mat around a ceramic seed vessel.

A tension line attached to the dry cradle is within contact at two seconds.
The recognisable young household member already holds the vessel's far side.
No card, date, narration or tutorial delays the touch.

#### `0:05–0:35` — Hold the whole load together

The user takes the line and changes pressure through one bounded direct
gesture. Hull angle, animal weight, water and the seed cradle answer
immediately. The young person corrects the wet mat as the user raises the
cradle away from the wash. Other hands secure tools and the animal line.

The current shifts once. The useful line moves rather than asking the user to
repeat the first gesture. Losing tension lowers the outer cradle and wets more
mat; correcting it raises the vessel. Water never destroys the seed. There is
no collision, death, restart or invisible score.

The dry grain can be heard moving inside the tilted ceramic. Two small granular
haptics follow the actual shift. A deeper resistance pulse belongs to the
hull's lean, not a success signal.

#### `0:35–0:55` — The route is larger than one boat

A worker takes the stabilising line only after the user's state holds. More
boats and wakes emerge through mist. Households, animals, baskets and tools are
read as moving work, never as an inventory display.

**Narration N01 — after precise input releases**

> Farming moved west with Anatolian households. They carried seed, livestock,
> tools, containers and the learned routines that made the parts work together.

#### `0:55–1:20` — The load reaches earth

The hull grounds. The user lifts the near weight of the seed vessel while the
young person and an adult carry the far side. Ceramic meets dry ground with one
low landing haptic. The grain sound clears as water recedes. The load is
received on the bank and precise input releases.

The household keeps moving while the user watches the actual vessel remain in
their hands. Animals, tools and containers pass into the same working space
before the vessel is offered back at prepared soil.

**Narration N02 — after the dry-bank transfer and input release**

> Repeated crossings carried the complete system onto Aegean shores and into
> Thessaly.

#### `1:20–2:05` — Make the carried seed answer

The user opens one resistant furrow. The young person steadies the vessel, then
covers the first grain after it falls. Grain changes from close ceramic
rasp to soft contact with soil.

The camera stays with the same strip. Weather and work move in two short
material passages: water darkens soil; feet and hands return; stalks rise and
bend. Neither passage exceeds eight seconds. People and animals remain at work
when the user regains contact.

#### `2:05–2:30` — Abundance closes into a limit

The user completes one forceful threshing action. The young person works among
the adults as real grain strikes, releases and gathers. That physical rhythm
briefly becomes the pulse of the whole scene.

At `2:18`, the grain fills the foreground and appears abundant. At `2:25`, the
last stalk is cleared away. The source acquires a finite edge. A food vessel by
the hearth, a raised loss reserve and a spring-seed vessel are already waiting
around it. The light outside has turned towards winter.

No narration explains sowing or threshing. The work pulse stops when the finite
edge becomes visible.

**Unfinished pressure:** The crossing delivered the system. The harvest now
has three claims and no second source.

**Causal transition:** The threshing surface never leaves the frame. The
question changes around the same grain rather than cutting to an allocation
screen.

**Cut gate:** The voyage ends as soon as vulnerability, shared labour and
repeated migration are clear. The season ends as soon as false abundance turns
finite. Remove any travel or harvest spectacle after those turns land.

### Sequence 02 — The harvest had to last

**Authored time:** `2:30–5:40`

**Place:** The same Thessalian household through autumn, winter and spring

**Principal interaction:**
`interaction-first-farmers-the-harvest-had-to-last` · `allocate`

**Historical obligation:** Make deferred consumption an immediate choice,
then make autumn provision answer a visible winter loss without inventing a
failure branch.

#### `2:30–2:42` — Three claims, one source

The camera is low enough to read grain volume, people and destinations at once.
No counters, shares, labels, tabs or destination cards divide the composition.

**Narration N03 — after all three claims are materially legible**

> Nothing more will ripen before winter. This harvest must feed the household,
> cover loss and preserve seed for spring.

#### `2:42–3:30` — Commit the autumn

The user makes a few substantial transfers from one visible source. Every
transfer changes the world:

- food settles near the hearth and enters present household work;
- reserve rises into a protected store built to absorb loss;
- spring grain enters the darkest vessel beside prepared tools and ground.

Quantised authority remains backstage. Volume, weight, sound and other
people's actions make the obligations understandable without arithmetic.

Each minimum has a material closure. An underfilled food vessel will not sit
securely, an underfilled reserve leaves its loss cloth slack, and insufficient
spring seed leaves part of the prepared furrow unanswered. Grain can be
reclaimed while any obligation is open.

Once all three minima hold, the remaining margin cannot strengthen every
future. The user's final placement leaves one destination visibly more robust
and another visibly thin. There is no perfect-solution badge and no false
promise that this choice can alter Europe's history.

#### `3:30–3:45` — One last revision

Before commitment, another worker lifts the weakest prepared container. Its
material response exposes the thin margin. The user may revise once or accept
it. Rain approaches, but neither time nor consumption advances before the
store is deliberately closed.

The spring vessel receives the quietest, firmest seal. Its granular sound
disappears completely.

#### `3:45–4:15` — Winter reaches the provision

Winter begins around the same composition. Food falls through use. Breath,
fire and rain replace the threshing pulse. The young member works beside the
adults; nobody waits theatrically for the user's decision to hurt them.

Water reaches a lower seam beneath the daily food container. Its sound changes
before the damage can be seen. At `4:15`, the user can act on the physical
breach.

#### `4:15–4:45` — Repair the loss

The user and the young person lift the wet container together. A fixed,
authored margin of grain has clumped and darkened. The user separates that
spoiled margin onto cloth, then opens the raised loss reserve and replaces what
winter food lost.

The autumn choice now answers materially. A stronger reserve retains a visible
remainder after replacement. A thinner reserve closes near its edge. Extra
food or spring seed remains where it was placed. The breach is representative,
not random, and never consumes the protected spring vessel.

#### `4:45–5:05` — The sealed future holds

The container settles. Work stops for five to six seconds. There is no music,
grain motif or interface response: only rain, breath, one drop and a hand on
sealed clay. The young person checks the same seal with an adult and withdraws.

**Narration N04 — after the silence**

> Grain eaten in winter cannot cover later loss or return to the soil. The
> household's autumn decision now governs spring.

#### `5:05–5:40` — The future makes a sound again

Spring light reaches the vessel. The user breaks the seal. Dry grain returns
to the soundscape before the lid is fully clear. The young person receives a
small amount and presses it into the furrow beside the user's action. The
granular haptic softens into earth.

**Narration N05 — after grain enters the furrow**

> The protected seed returns to worked soil instead of the hearth.

The young person helps close surviving seed into a newly prepared travel sack.
This completes their continuous role. The first household does not continue
unchanged through later centuries.

**Natural exit:** Spring sowing is the first complete stopping place. Exact
state is already saved. No episode card, recovery control or visible break
appears.

**Unfinished pressure:** One household has carried the system through a year.
Later households must carry it into a river world that already knows its own
routes, foods and ground.

**Causal transition:** The travel sack enters a boat. Fast, honest handoffs
change people, sacks, boats and dates while preserving the route and function.
No object or person silently crosses the interval.

**Cut gate:** This remains the longest sustained dilemma. Every transfer must
change an obligation; every winter beat must reveal cause or repair. Cut any
grain movement that merely repeats handling.

### Sequence 03 — The river already knows the landing

**Authored time:** `5:40–7:20`

**Place:** The Iron Gates, 6200–5800 BC

**Principal interaction:**
`interaction-first-farmers-at-the-iron-gates` · `transform`

**Historical obligation:** Make the inhabited river world active before
incoming farming cargo arrives, and make local knowledge necessary to the
landing rather than presenting contact as an exchange display.

#### `5:40–5:55` — Later hands, same obligation

The spring departure becomes three quick, readable handoffs through different
hands, new sacks and changed boats. Each exchange is complete before identity
changes. The last boat enters the Iron Gates current with a visibly different
household and container.

#### `5:55–6:08` — The bank exists first

Before cargo touches shore, nets under load, smoke, houses, graves and a worn
landing stone establish repeated river use. Fish strike wet wood. A local
worker reads the current with a guide pole while the arriving hull is already
being pushed past the landing.

#### `6:08–6:25` — Accept local pressure

The local worker places the pole into a known lee and offers a loaded line.
The user takes its pressure and pivots the hull around the current rather than
pulling straight at the bank. The local pole and user-held line must work
together. Releasing returns the boat to a safe outer arc; it never wrecks.

The hull grounds only when both forces align. The action proves dependence
before narration can name contact.

#### `6:25–6:55` — Move what each side can carry

The user passes one heavy grain-and-pottery load onto the worn landing while
river workers steady an animal and receive other material. A different young
person at the bank uses the familiar two-handed support gesture on a newly
made vessel.

Fish, hide and stone are then secured for movement along the same working
route. Surrounding people perform the rest. The user experiences weight in
both directions without completing a cargo checklist.

#### `6:55–7:20` — The route changes without becoming new

The line releases. Time moves briefly around the fixed landing stone. Worked
ground and houses change; nets, boats, graves and river food remain active.
People and practices cross at different rates. A later household is already
moving inland before the explanation ends.

**Narration N06 — after precise input releases**

> At the Iron Gates, incoming farmers met established river communities.
> People and practices crossed through older routes, while river foods and
> local ancestry persisted unevenly.

**Sound and haptics:** The river bed stays sample-continuous. Current pressure,
guide-pole bite, wet rope, ceramic, fish, hide, stone and animal step remain
separate. The grain pattern is recognisable inside a different vessel with a
different resonance.

**Unfinished pressure:** The landing works because knowledge and material move
through an occupied place. The changed route now brings a household to ground
where shelter must carry more than one lifetime.

**Causal transition:** The camera follows the worn route. Newly made seed
sacks and tools move through visible handoffs and are set beside prepared post
cuts on loess ground. A worker then places an actual tensioned lifting line in
the user's hand while the frame is already under load. There is no black
screen or title-card reset.

**Cut gate:** The sequence must read as one difficult shared landing, two
directions of material movement and an immediate departure. If it reads as a
list or museum demonstration, give more work to surrounding people and cut.

### Sequence 04 — Hold the house

**Authored time:** `7:20–9:50`

**Place:** The Danube and loess plains, 5600–4900 BC

**Principal interaction:**
`interaction-first-farmers-the-house-outlives` · `assemble`

**Historical obligation:** Make inherited ground emerge from one continuous
load that is raised, inhabited, weakened and transferred to new timber and new
people.

#### `7:20–8:00` — Raise the load

The route arrives at prepared post cuts during a collective lift. A different
young worker holds a wedge beside the principal frame. The structure is
already under ordinary heavy load when the user takes the tension line.

The user raises the frame with visible workers. One footing enters skewed and
the load pulls sideways. Correcting the line lets the young worker place the
wedge and guides the post into its cut. Releasing too early lowers the frame to
a safe, unfinished rest. Nothing collapses and no person waits beneath danger.

Earth compresses. Timber flexes. The frame takes weight.

#### `8:00–8:15` — Shelter pays, then begins to fail

The household completes roof, hearth and raised store as consequences of the
load-bearing frame, not as three placement tasks. Rain strikes outside while
people, grain and fire remain dry within. Before that protection can become a
completion tableau, rain darkens timber, thatch changes and the same load point
starts to groan beneath a sagging post.

#### `8:15–8:55` — Transfer the weight

The user catches the familiar line and supports the roof load. Other workers
brace a new post. While tension is held, the rotten timber releases from the
ground and the replacement is guided beside its old mark.

The action is one load transfer. Holding too little returns the roof to a safe
sag and keeps the old support engaged. When the new post seats, the roof line
rises, the smoke opening clears and the store remains dry.

#### `8:55–9:20` — The place answers while pressure returns

Time stays at the load point. At a later repair, a visibly different adult
holds the same support while a new young worker repeats the wedge gesture.
Bodies, clothes, rope and timber have changed. The threshold, well, field,
graves and old postholes remain in the same ground.

The building sound stops for four seconds. Only earth settling, wind through
the opening and one human breath remain. Then vessels and bodies begin to fill
the house before the quiet has become rest.

**Narration N07 — after the silence, while crowding becomes visible**

> Timber failed; the threshold, worked ground, graves and postholes remained.
> In some communities, fathers and sons held strongly to these inherited
> places, while women often arrived from neighbouring groups.

#### `9:20–9:50` — Continuity forces a departure

More sleeping places, vessels, bodies and animals now crowd the inherited
plot. The narration ends as animals load the enclosure and block the threshold.
The user helps clear the real opening, then takes the near weight of a newly
made seed basket while a departing household carries its tools through. The
recurring two-handed gesture now belongs to another person and another vessel.

**Unfinished pressure:** The house survived. Because it survived, it can no
longer hold everyone who depends on its ground.

**Causal transition:** The supported basket and departing people pass directly
through the threshold into the herd lane and wet ground of the next action.

**Cut gate:** The user performs two turns on one load: raise it, then transfer
it from failing timber. Intermediate construction belongs to the household.
If shelter becomes a build puzzle or the time passage becomes a montage, cut.

### Sequence 05 — The house cannot hold everyone

**Authored time:** `9:50–12:00`

**Place:** The Danube and loess belt, 5500–4000 BC

**Principal interaction:**
`interaction-first-farmers-more-mouths-more-land` · `transform`

**Historical obligation:** Make demographic and ecological expansion the
direct physical consequence of household continuity, then show local
contraction without stopping the larger movement.

#### `9:50–10:10` — Open the crowded plot

Other hands take the basket forward and open the enclosure against the
animals' weight. The user takes the forward animal line as the first bodies
enter the route. Population does not emerge from a slider or a gate tap.

#### `10:10–10:45` — Find usable ground through movement

The user guides the first animals towards water. Wet ground takes weight,
slope turns bodies and closed woodland denies a direct line. Animals hesitate
and bunch at poor ground; they respond when a viable route opens. No arrow or
painted path chooses for them.

The herd's actual movement wears the beginning of a lane. People carrying the
complete system follow it.

#### `10:45–11:10` — Fix the next field edge

Workers clear and sow beside water. Axes remain in human hands; trees never
fall beneath a magic finger. The user draws the final physical boundary into a
prepared earth slot and drives its loaded end stake only when field, herd,
store and household are operating together.

The seated boundary is a local seal, not an instruction that makes population
appear.

#### `11:10–11:22` — Success is already moving on

Paths deepen. A store rises. New hearths and fields appear through later
people's work. The established settlement remains visible behind while the
new one grows ahead. Animals and another household are already passing the
new field edge when smoke stops at the exact clearing the user opened.

#### `11:22–11:40` — Cross the ground that emptied

The camera remains inside that movement and reaches the abandoned clearing
within seconds. Its enclosure gap stands open. Rain enters through roof
material. The field edge softens and young trees stand in worked soil. No
disease, conflict or crop failure is invented as a single explanation.

Human sound disappears from this place while work remains audible beyond it.
A later household is already crossing the cold hearth with a new basket,
animals and tools.

**Narration N08 — before the user takes the next load**

> More households required more fields and herd lanes. Some clearings emptied
> and returned to grass and young trees; the carried system continued.

#### `11:40–12:00` — Take the movement through the loss

The user takes the basket's near weight as it crosses the cold hearth. The
empty clearing stays large behind the moving hands. The load enters a boat
already under pressure, and the final principal interaction begins before a
new scene can establish itself.

**Unfinished pressure:** No single settlement contains the result. The route,
store, field, inherited plot and herd lane now have to be read together.

**Causal transition:** The user does not release the new load between the cold
hearth and the first continental threshold.

**Cut gate:** The new clearing must grow out of crowded bodies and animal
movement. The abandoned ground must be encountered at working distance. If
either becomes an overview or narrated statistic, rebuild the transition.

### Sequence 06 — The relay becomes a continent

**Authored time:** `12:00–13:35`

**Place:** Europe, 4500–3300 BC

**Principal interaction:**
`interaction-first-farmers-a-continent-remade` · `transform`

**Historical obligation:** Make the final principal interaction one pressured
local act in which the complete system has to hold together. Let the
continental scale emerge from the already persistent world, without three
tutorial stations, a callback montage or a magical map.

#### `12:00–12:12` — The relay changes hands

Hands on the Danube bank receive the basket from the user and precise input
releases. The route remains in motion. Around fixed ground and honest visible
handoffs, later people lift a new basket away from damp and place it in a
raised store. People, container and date change; function and route continue.

These actions belong to surrounding households. The user is not asked to
repeat the crossing and storage interactions as two abbreviated museums.

#### `12:12–12:42` — Hold the complete system together

At the moving edge of settlement, a household reaches usable ground beside
water. Its seed, tools and raised storage are already being carried into place
when animals load a flexible barrier and begin to turn towards the sown strip.

The user takes the barrier line. Its resistance changes as animal bodies pass.
The useful angle must keep the herd lane open to water while protecting the
worked soil. Other people bring seed and tools through the opening, secure dry
storage and guide the rear animals. The user cannot complete their work and
they cannot close the coupled system while the line is wrongly loaded.

Releasing returns the barrier to a safe partial closure. There is no timer,
stampede or fail state. Once household, seed, herd, field and store are all
physically present, the user anchors the line to the real gate. One local
system holds.

No narration, date label or location card interrupts the action.

#### `12:42–13:00` — All prior causes occupy one living world

The gate settles as the camera is already following the herd lane into the
active valley. Nothing lights up in sequence and no earlier interaction is
replayed. Route, dry storage, worked soil, rebuilt plots, occupied houses and
regrowth are simultaneously active conditions of the same connected world.

The Iron Gates landing and the other bounded world cells keep their persistent
state; the finale does not teleport them into one impossible view. Their routes
and material work continue into the landscape now being followed. The empty
clearing remains close enough to prevent a uniform triumphal result.

#### `13:00–13:18` — Rise only as far as the evidence permits

The camera rises in two moves shorter than eight seconds and settles between
them on living 3D work. Fields, stores, animals, paths, houses, forest and
regrowth remain legible. Spatial work sounds accumulate asynchronously: grain
against new containers, hoof in lane, post against earth, hearth and footfall.

**Narration N09 — only after the connected condition is visible**

> By 3300 BC, Europe had become a continent of fields, herds and long-lived
> settlements.

#### `13:18–13:35` — The settled world remains exposed

The camera returns towards one active raised store. Farming work continues in
the foreground. The eastern horizon opens into grass and a low material mobile
rhythm enters. It contains no cavalry, saddles, stirrups, swords, chariots,
battle pose or victory sting.

**Narration N10 — after the eastern edge enters**

> The steppe would meet fields to seize, stores to command, houses to inherit
> and paternal lines to reorder.

Control returns while the world remains active. There is no completion card,
reward summary or interface covering the result.

**End state:** Route, furrow, store, landing, postholes, threshold, field,
herd lane, regrowth and farming belt remain deterministic addressable state.

**Cut gate:** The final relay must accelerate and the landscape must already
contain its conclusion. If the camera or narration supplies scale that the
persistent world cannot prove, rebuild the world state before producing art.

### Haptic grammar

Haptics remain bound to the approved interaction patterns. They report contact,
material resistance, transfer and closure; they never become a reward layer or
continuous vibration.

| Principal interaction | Existing pattern |

3. a `0:00–7:20` pressure test of crossing, harvest, winter and Iron Gates;
