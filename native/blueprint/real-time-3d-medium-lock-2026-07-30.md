# Journey medium decision — authored real-time 3D

Status: `APPROVED_BY_EDITOR_IN_CHIEF`

Decision date: 30 July 2026

Scope: the complete native Journey, including all 24 launch chapters

Reopen authority: editor-in-chief only

## Locked decision

The Journey is produced as an authored real-time 3D experience. Real 3D is the
authoritative substrate for space, historical action, time, material response
and persistent consequence throughout the app. The quality ambition is
unchanged. A production requirement up to roughly three to four times the
previous 2.5D estimate is accepted when it is needed to reach that ambition.

The lock concerns the medium, not a particular engine. The engine and asset
toolchain remain implementation decisions and must be selected by a measured
Chapter 01 proof on the supported iPhone performance class.

## What the lock requires

- Anything the user acts on, and anything that carries the result of that
  action, exists as inspectable 3D geometry, material and deterministic state.
- Camera, staging, traversal and timing are authored. A chapter can stream
  bounded scene volumes while feeling like one uninterrupted historical
  journey.
- Historical transitions grow out of an action, a material, a place or a
  persistent trace. Cards, reading pages and generic next controls cannot be
  the connective tissue.
- The 3D source, scene state, interaction bindings and build inputs remain
  versionable and operable through the Codex-controlled workflow.
- The complete experience, including exact restoration, remains available
  offline after installation.

## What the lock does not add

- no freely navigable open world;
- no player avatar, joystick or generic locomotion layer;
- no branching alternative history;
- no combat, inventory economy, crafting tree, NPC simulation or generic
  physics sandbox;
- no demand for close-up dialogue scenes, lip sync or cinematic coverage that
  does not teach the approved causal story.

These exclusions preserve authored focus and keep the increased production
inside the parts the user can feel.

## Effect on existing work

Existing 2.5D prototypes, stills, masks, depth plans and compositor work may be
retained as composition references, interaction evidence or disposable
technical research. They are not the shipping visual foundation. No further
production asset may be commissioned from the old layered-scene plan.

The active visual work packages in `native/PRODUCTION_PLAN.md` and
`native/WORK_BREAKDOWN.md` are rebased to the Chapter 01 real-time 3D proof.
Their non-visual platform, offline, restoration, editorial and release gates
remain in force. The former 2.5D asset steps survive only as explicitly marked
prototype evidence and git history.
