# Frozen iPhone production rules

## Status — 5 August 2026

The iPhone project in this directory is frozen and inactive. Preserve all files. Do not continue,
redesign, refactor, regenerate, validate for promotion or otherwise advance this product unless the
user explicitly reopens it. Read-only inspection remains allowed.

Nothing in this directory has design or architectural authority over the new Mac exploration. The
website is its sole starting point, and any reuse from this archive requires a fresh, explicit decision.
The remaining rules document the frozen iPhone project and apply only if that project is reopened.

The repository-root `AGENTS.md` and `site/AGENTS.md` apply in full. Read both
before changing anything below this directory.

Before writing a native chapter manuscript, scene flow, interaction sequence or
chapter implementation, read [`bibles/experience-bible.md`](./bibles/experience-bible.md).
It is the shared authority for complete experiential flow. A native chapter may
not regress to pictures, prose and audio joined by small interactions; source
prose defines what the enacted historical world must teach.

- The user is editor-in-chief. An approved chapter contract cannot be changed
  by research, verification, code generation or implementation work.
- Public content and backstage research are separate source trees. A shipping
  package must never contain sources, evidence records, confidence data,
  historiography, counterarguments, methodology or verifier findings.
- The native app is authored historical nonfiction. Do not add quizzes, XP,
  streaks, collectible systems, alternative timelines or generic widgets.
- The five principal interaction grammars are `trace`, `allocate`, `assemble`,
  `pressure` and `transform`. Every principal interaction must produce a
  persistent historical consequence.
- The app and every installed content package must work without a network.
  Purchase, restore, release discovery and initial package delivery are the
  only network edges.
- No external humans and no incremental pre-revenue cost. A tool or asset with
  a billing requirement, unclear commercial rights or a non-commercial licence
  fails the production gate.
- Codex is the only authoring surface. Keep authoritative state in reviewable,
  versioned text and asset files; never require manual work in an opaque editor.

## Chapter 01 production ratchet

- Chapter 01 follows
  [`production/3d/chapter01/VISUAL_COMPLETION_GOAL.md`](./production/3d/chapter01/VISUAL_COMPLETION_GOAL.md)
  through five ordered scopes: a short non-promoting method proof, the complete
  five-cell and six-sequence greybox using the current 34-beat map as a
  measured editing hypothesis, the complete `0:00–2:30`
  production slice, production of the remaining chapter after that slice
  passes, and one final whole-chapter gate. No second-by-second or cumulative
  `V1`–`V9` promotion ladder may be reintroduced.
- `first-farmers-3d-review-v1` is a non-shipping technical continuity proof,
  never a visual candidate. Its visible people, animals, environments and
  materials are not production assets and may not be promoted through polish.
- The complete greybox must prove the approved sequence order, six interaction
  identities and `WorldEffect`s, local causal comprehension, model-QA,
  modality-adapted accessibility, offline use and restoration from the last
  durable checkpoint before final art begins. The approved Chapter 01 cue text
  remains unchanged; measured timing may produce a private cut or placement
  proposal, but any public-wording change requires editor-in-chief approval. The
  `13:35` map, 34-beat ledger and every beat-level number are measured
  calibration hypotheses, not runtime quotas. Beats may be merged, rebuilt or
  cut when the locked causal obligations remain intact.
- The first production-quality gate is the complete `0:00–2:30` opening from
  `cross-current` through the stable `finite-harvest` state. It must prove the
  same seed vessel across the Aegean–Thessaly handoff, direct touch, shared
  human work, material consequence, final-oriented sound and durable-checkpoint
  restore. Do not produce final art for the remaining runtime before this
  complete slice passes editor and physical-device review.
- After the slice passes, the remaining cells and beats may enter production.
  Visual completion still requires the complete signed chapter, its real road
  entry, one uninterrupted run, natural split runs, pause and return, all
  accessibility modalities, offline use and restoration to pass together on
  the registered physical iPhone with explicit editor approval.
- A motivated cut or authored time transition is allowed when the causal
  carrier, handoff and new identity remain clear. It may not conceal loading,
  an asset swap or an impossible surviving person or object.
- Chapter 01 active play has one persistent, visually quiet 44-point pause
  control. It opens `Resume`, sound/narration, captions and `Back to the road`.
  Leaving returns to a focused Chapter 01 road anchor with an immediate,
  saved-state `Resume`; a hard kill resumes from the last durable checkpoint
  without repeating a committed `WorldEffect`. Ellipsis controls and hidden
  tap-to-reveal navigation are rejected.
