The editor-in-chief approved all four factual repairs for the frozen
37-segment review manuscript. Each repair is applied without changing the

neither final public-wording approval nor production or shipping approval.

## 1. Seed reserve and sowing season — F5

Finding: `ff-finding-thessaly-sowing-season`

Decision: approved by the editor-in-chief and applied for Chapter 01 review.

Keep the allocation mechanism. Rename the public destination from `Spring
seed` to `Seed grain`. Replace the spring and thaw cues with the interval from
harvest through winter to the next sowing, using autumn rain where a visible
seasonal trigger is needed.

The governing sentence becomes:

> Seed grain is food refused in winter: the next field held inside the present harvest.

Stable IDs may retain `spring-seed`; IDs are not public wording.

## 2. What outlives the builders — F3

Finding: `ff-finding-physical-house-outlives-builders`

Decision: approved by the editor-in-chief and applied for Chapter 01 review.

Move continuity from one timber building to the inherited plot and rebuilding
sequence. Replace:

> The next structure must outlast its builders.

with:

> The next house plot must outlast its first timbers.

The house, inheritance and later-steppe handoff remain unchanged.

## 3. Longhouse assembly order — F2

Finding: `ff-finding-longhouse-universal-build-order`

Decision: approved by the editor-in-chief and applied for Chapter 01 review.

Retain the `Assemble` grammar and make the recovered structural dependency the
only forced order. Posts come first. Hearth, storage and roof become available
once the frame stands; the user may place those three in any order.

Replace:

> Assemble the house in causal order.

with:

> Raise the load-bearing frame. Then make the shelter fit for a household.

Resistance applies to an unsupported frame, not to an asserted universal
sequence for fitting the interior and roof.

## 4. The first farmers' languages — F3

Finding: `ff-finding-first-farmer-languages-gone`

Decision: approved by the editor-in-chief and applied for Chapter 01 review.

Preserve the ending's contrast without claiming extinction of every language
lineage. Replace:

> The languages of the first farming households are gone. Their structure remains.

with:

> Their voices were never written down. Their structure remains.

The next sentence continues directly with seed, store, field and rebuilt
house. No public qualification or competing account is added.
