## Historical action

The user handles four parts of one household system:

1. Posts enter prepared ground and take the frame.
2. Hearth material reaches the centre after the posts hold.
3. Storage enters the dry bay after the posts hold.
4. Roofing fibre closes the shelter after the current authored dependencies
   are satisfied.

Contact, lift, carry, target approach, resistance and snap-back remain
ephemeral. The durable Journey receives only a reducer-accepted `place` action.
The final consequence is the plot: timber fails, while the threshold, worked
ground and old post marks receive another frame.

The current strict dependency graph remains data authority. The art must also
support a later data-only change in hearth, storage or roof order. No base
plate may bake one unresolved dependency choice into the image.

## Selected composition

Option A4, `The Inherited Ground`, is the provisional composition base. The
camera begins at posthole level and looks along the house axis. Loose timber,
hearth material, storage vessels and roofing fibre occupy reachable positions
inside one forest-bounded clearing. Accepted posts lead the eye into the
frame; the ground must show that another house already stood here.

The next visual pass must make that inherited plot fully legible at phone
scale without dispersing the interaction field:

- one offset older footprint formed by darker filled post marks;
- a reworn threshold and inherited approach path;
- old marks, current open holes and packed accepted posts as distinct earth
  states;
- one clearing bounded by substantial woodland beyond the settlement;
- no glowing sockets, outlines, labels or archaeological-display treatment.

The closest post setting must show wedges, tamped loess and displaced soil as
the post settles. The visible structure must carry weight from ground through
frame to roof. It cannot resemble a modern trussed barn.

- Master canvas: at least `1290 × 2796` pixels.
- Orientation: iPhone portrait only.
- Finished overscan: at least 15 percent around every supported camera crop.
- Camera: one restrained editorial rail from inherited ground through accepted
  parts into the completed interior.
- Reduce Motion: two authored static compositions, one for the completed
  assembly and one for the rebuilt plot.
- No free camera and no landscape derivative used as a master.

Every normal-blended moving layer requires a measured rail-bounded
disocclusion underlay. Screen or light layers cannot hide missing reconstruction.
State variants change only inside their exact registered authorisation masks.

## Material states

The scene must be able to render these combinations without a global redraw:

- bare inherited plot;
- posts available;
- posts packed and frame bearing;
- hearth available and accepted;
- storage available and accepted;
- hearth accepted before storage;
- storage accepted before hearth;
- roof available;
- roof settled;
- timber aged and failed;
- rebuilt frame beside the surviving old marks.

The unplaced source disappears only when the corresponding accepted state is
durable. A cancelled or rejected carry returns to the source bytes. No open
posthole, packed post, hearth, vessel, wall or roof bundle may be painted into a
global base if a later state must remove it.

## Direct manipulation

The source material and every target slot receive a minimum 44-point effective
hit envelope at the smallest supported crop. The visible object need not grow
to that size; contact feedback must make the acquired material unmistakable.

The response sequence is:

`contact → lift → carry → slot approach → resistance or accepted bearing → durable consequence`

Wrong-slot and dependency-rejected drops show physical resistance and return
the material. They append no progress event. Accepted placement settles only
after the durable commit. VoiceOver invokes the identical `place` action and
reaches the same final hash.

## Light, motion and sound

`Darkness frames the evidence` remains the franchise rule. Here the clearest
light belongs to compressed timber, packed earth and the hearth that has become
viable inside the frame. The scene cannot repeat Harvest's bright finite mound.

Living motion comes from timber flex, settling earth, daub dust, fibre drag,
small fire, smoke and ordinary work. Resistance is material: a post tilts, a
roof bundle catches, earth refuses an unprepared load. There is no bounce,
spark, icon pulse or success flare.

The existing Longhouse responsive program supplies contact, drag, resistance
and consequence beds. Its retained deterministic render remains provisional;
its runtime provenance is rebound to the current controller and transport
hashes. Narration remains absent until the separate voice gate.
