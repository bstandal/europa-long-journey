# Chapter 01 enhanced continuity visual review

Review date: 2026-07-30

Classification: `GREYBOX_CONTINUITY_CANDIDATE`

Gate: `finalArtGate: OPEN`

Visual QA result: **FAIL for simulator-candidate integration.**

The nine 720×1280 previews confirm that the skinned hero and cattle candidates
resolve in all five composed cells. They replace hidden stand-in meshes while
the original person, animal and canonical runtime roots remain unchanged. The
review rig improves causal framing compared with the first pass, but the world
art, poses and contacts remain visibly greybox.

## Cell observations

- **Aegean crossing:** The blown-out sky and nearest gunwale occlusion are
  removed. The seed keeper now reads beside the carrier and the young cattle
  rig replaces the primitive bovine. The line still crosses the action group;
  hands do not grip it and the third worker is not legible in the two review
  frames.
- **Thessaly:** The seed carrier and preserved bowl read as one action group.
  The grinder, grain mass and seed vessel are visible together. The grinder
  still needs a dedicated kneeling deformation and real quern contact.
- **Iron Gates:** The wide view shows the line connecting boat and bank. The
  reverse view makes the resident and receiver readable face-on. The pale bank
  occupies too much of the frame, and static open hands do not communicate the
  knowledge handoff or line transfer.
- **Longhouse:** Separate raising and pulling framings now expose the workers
  and active post instead of the central occluder. Wattle and roof geometry
  remain schematic, and neither worker closes on the post or line.
- **Settlement:** The cattle library removes the balloon-bodied stand-ins and
  preserves both herd states. Herd anatomy, variation and hoof contact remain
  candidate quality. The final-state worker is fully visible, but fastening
  contact and barrier construction are unresolved.

## Open visual gates

- Faces, hair silhouettes, hands, feet and garment deformation remain candidate
  quality.
- The current shared standing gestures do not communicate each historical task
  strongly enough for final silent comprehension.
- Exact hand-to-tool, hand-to-vessel, foot-to-ground and body-to-environment
  contact remains open.
- The source cells are continuity geometry with placeholder lighting and
  materials; these previews are not evidence of final environment quality.
- The action cannot yet pass silent-comprehension review in Aegean, Iron Gates
  or the longhouse without outside explanation.
- Physical iPhone rendering, performance, thermal and haptic review has not
  been performed here.

Rights are not an open gate for this candidate. The pinned character provenance
records `rightsGate: PASS` and the garment topology is project-authored.
