# Native benchmark set — 23 July 2026

This is a production control sheet, not public positioning. Availability,
store presentation and product behaviour must be rechecked at the complete
Chapter 01 slice, content-complete build and release candidate.

The linked US App Store listings were rechecked on 23 July 2026. Store metrics
are a dated snapshot, never a permanent product claim.

## Products to beat

| Product | What it sets the bar for | EUROCENTRIC must demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paladin: Learn History](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paladin-learn-history/id6511242367) | Immediate entry; five-minute animated lessons; cinematic video slides with narration and soundtrack; quizzes, levels and collectible figures; 16K US ratings at 4.6 in the dated snapshot | Greater historical depth; touch that changes causal mechanisms rather than testing recall; exact interruption recovery; one coherent civilisation-scale world whose earlier consequences remain visible |
| [Monument Valley 3](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/monument-valley-3/id6443563379) | Authored touch, visual coherence, legible motion and a free opening followed by one permanent unlock | Equally clear touch language and scene-to-scene finish across all 24 chapters; historical action must remain intelligible without tutorial copy |
| [The Room: Old Sins](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-room-old-sins/id1286676015) | Tactile object response, material presence, atmospheric audio and sustained curiosity | Comparable immediacy of touch, resistance, sound and consequence while every mechanism remains historically meaningful |
| [Inua — A Story in Ice and Time](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/inua-a-story-in-ice-and-time/id1562868713) | Narrative movement across periods and spatially staged historical scenes | A clearer fixed historical spine, stronger cumulative continuity and a living world that remembers the user’s completed actions |
| [Audible](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/audible-audiobooks-podcasts/id379693831) | Long-form narration, offline listening, durable position and read/listen continuity | Narration and reading must share one canonical script; controlled pause must restore exactly and hard termination within 250 ms |
| [Atlas Obscura Travel Guide](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/atlas-obscura-travel-guide/id1563250221) | A map as the primary discovery surface and place-led return behaviour | A more directed cartographic world where places, routes and institutions change through historical causation instead of remaining independent entries |
| [Wikipedia](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wikipedia/id324715238) | Distraction-free native reading, broad accessibility and an ad-free knowledge product | Stronger authored sequence and immersion without surrendering readability, Dynamic Type or VoiceOver parity |

## Comparison gates

Score each line with concrete captured evidence: `LEADS`, `PARITY`, `DEFICIT`
or `NOT TESTED`. A score without a build, screen recording, audio cue or test
result is invalid.

1. Time from cold launch to first historically meaningful action.
2. Touch-response latency and the material specificity of feedback.
3. Whether interaction reveals a mechanism that prose alone could not.
4. Narrative pressure through an uninterrupted 8–15 minute arc.
5. Visual composition, motion restraint, legibility and period specificity.
6. Narrator performance, score, soundscape, silence and haptic direction.
7. Exact interruption and restoration, including spoken position.
8. Offline completeness after installation.
9. Map/world continuity and persistent consequences between chapters.
10. VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Increased Contrast and Reduce Motion parity.
11. Free-to-paid transition without interrupting a dramatic arc.
12. Quality of the weakest required chapter.

## Passing rule

- Chapter 01 may scale only when EUROCENTRIC leads Paladin on causal
  interaction, narrative command and cumulative world; reaches parity with
  The Room on tactile response; and reaches parity with Audible on offline
  position restoration.
- Content-complete fails if any chapter records a core `DEFICIT` or if the
  benchmark cannot identify why the same interaction belongs to that specific
  chapter.
- Release candidate fails while any dimension is `NOT TESTED`, while the
  weakest chapter falls below the strongest product comparison relevant to it,
  or while polish is concentrated in the opening and free route.
