# Continent Remade responsive audio sources

This directory holds the editable, provisional score and soundscape sources for
`A Continent Remade`. They are backstage production material and cannot enter a
shipping package in their present state.

The score joins material already established inside *The First Farmers*. The
Harvest open fifth and returning-field figure meet the Longhouse hearth and
held-house figures, then form one restrained farming-belt statement. The three
stems keep ground, household and visible work independently editable. Density
increases as the coupled household system spreads, without a march, trailer
pulse or triumphal swell.

The soundscape uses only the five existing project-authored local DSP
algorithms. Rain on worked ground, settlement hearths, stored grain, household
fibre and visible field or woodland work provide concrete sources for every
sound. Rain is not relabelled as a river, and material work is not relabelled as
animal sound. No field recording, generated finished track or network service
is required.

The source contract contains five regions: a finite 48-second approach, three
sample-aligned 15-second interaction beds and a finite 45-second consequence.
Every region has three score stems, five independently rendered material layers
and one named silence window. The intended runtime program keeps narration and
timed haptics out of its loop beds; the Transform reducer remains responsible
for `drag`, `break` and the durable `seal`.

Both JSON documents remain `PROVISIONAL_NON_SHIPPING` with shipping explicitly
`PROHIBITED`. They provide no artistic, editor, physical-device or shipping
approval. Work-object projection, deterministic double rendering, receipt
generation, runtime binding and integrated narration calibration remain later
gates.
