# More Mouths responsive audio sources

This directory holds the editable score and soundscape sources for `More
Mouths`. The sources and generated work object remain provisional and are
excluded from shipping packages.

The Harvest open fifth and returning-field figure meet the Longhouse hearth and
held-house figures. The three stems keep ground, household and visible work
independently editable. Density increases as households open ground. The
culminating `farming-belt-held` motif belongs to `A Continent Remade` and is
forbidden here.

The soundscape binds settlement hearths, stored grain, household fibre,
clearing work and herd lanes to visible sources. `herd-ground-movement-v1`
produces clustered hoof contact, low group weight shifts and restrained
breath/body movement. It cannot produce species calls, bells, tack, gallop or
stampede. No recorded animal source or network service is used.

The source contract contains five regions: a finite 48-second approach, three
sample-aligned 15-second interaction beds and a finite 45-second consequence.
Every region has three score stems, five independently rendered material layers
and one named silence window. Narration and timed haptics stay out of loop beds.
The Transform reducer owns `drag`, threshold `break` and the durable `seal`.

Both JSON documents remain `PROVISIONAL_NON_SHIPPING` with shipping explicitly
`PROHIBITED`. Artistic, editor, physical-device and shipping approval remain
open. Integrated narration calibration waits for the selected narration master.
