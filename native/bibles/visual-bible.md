# Native visual bible

Status: locked production rule, rebased to authored real-time 3D on 30 July
2026. The user approves chapter-specific directions through each editorial
contract; this file controls the shared visual floor.

[`experience-bible.md`](./experience-bible.md) controls complete chapter flow,
causal carriers and human or institutional continuity. This file controls how
that experience exists and remains legible in authored 3D.

## Governing composition

The selected world direction is **The Cartographic Procession**. Europe is one
continuous material world whose roads, borders, cities, institutions and
objects accumulate through time. A chapter does not open as a card or leave
the user in a library grid. The world moves to the next historical pressure.

The display is iPhone portrait only. The baseline canvas is 393 × 852 points.
Every authored camera and interaction envelope must preserve causal focus,
reachable material and readable framing across the supported portrait canvases.
The 3D proof sets the required world margin, field of view and safe regions;
there is no inherited overscan percentage from the former plate workflow.

`Darkness frames the evidence` is literal art direction:

- near-black creates the frame, depth and separation;
- readable midtones show faces, hands, tools, fabric, stone and action;
- the mechanism that changes history carries the clearest light;
- darkness may never conceal a detail the scene asks the user to understand;
- gold and brightness belong to named material or light, never generic prestige.

## Scene construction

Existing web plates are composition and art-direction references. Their pixels,
estimated depth and generated anatomy are not production scene data. A native
world cell requires:

1. a versioned scene graph with stable object and state IDs;
2. modelled geometry with authored topology, scale, transforms and UVs;
3. material and texture sources with a declared colour and compression path;
4. rigged or state-authored motion for every person, animal and mechanism that
   must change;
5. physically coherent lighting, shadow, atmosphere, water and weather states;
6. authored camera rails, anchors, focus volumes and portrait safe regions;
7. semantic contact, collision, manipulation and causal-state bindings;
8. deterministic Reduce Motion states that preserve spatial order and result;
9. LOD, culling, streaming, memory and restoration declarations;
10. provenance, edit history, hashes and package-relative assets bound to the
    signed manifest before decode.

Block the complete causal movement in 3D before producing final art. Judge
geometry, people, material and light through the actual authored cameras and
interactions. Independently produced assets that drift in scale, identity,
perspective, anatomy, period or light are rebuilt. No opaque editor file may be
the only authoritative source of a scene.

## Transition integrity

- A place, time or scale transition visually follows its declared person,
  object, document, load, route, surface, practice or world trace.
- A long interval uses visible handoffs and newly made material. No face,
  costume, voice or prop survives beyond a plausible identity merely to hide a
  stream or cut.
- Time advances around stable surviving geometry or state. Weather, wear, work,
  succession and changed authority establish the interval before a date label.
- A camera move may reveal an accumulated result; it cannot create the result,
  replace a missing causal connection or fire earlier traces as a recap
  montage.

## Motion

Motion has one of three jobs: establish human presence, respond to action or
make consequence visible. Decorative idle motion cannot carry a scene.

- Camera movement follows authored rails and cannot become free navigation.
- Parallax, occlusion and perspective arise from the same 3D geometry the
  interaction uses. Camera movement cannot manufacture a causal change.
- Atmosphere is spatially coherent and tied to a source: wind, smoke, dust,
  water, flame, textile, work or crowds.
- A completed consequence remains in the scene or living world; it does not
  reset when explanatory copy has finished.
- Reduce Motion replaces travel with fixed anchors and restrained state
  transitions while preserving real spatial order. It never removes or falsely
  unoccludes the causal result.
- Stable visual variants come only from accepted reducer state. Gesture contact,
  carried material and resistance are transient responses and cannot forge a
  completed historical state.
- Direct material remains bound to stable world transforms from source through
  transfer and destination. A camera rail cannot make a path drift away from
  the physical object it joins.

The first 3D continuity proof establishes camera velocity, acceleration,
rotation and motion-comfort limits on device. No limit from the former
point-displacement compositor carries forward as a target.

## Typography and interface

- Bodoni Moda is the display face for franchise and chapter scale.
- Manrope is the working face for body, controls, captions and metadata.
- Historical labels name the action: `Carry the household`, `Raise the road`,
  `Hold the frontier`. Avoid `Continue`, `Explore interaction` and tutorial
  language when the historical action can carry the control.
- Text remains native interface text. Do not bake narrative or labels into art
  or textures.
- All body text scales through accessibility sizes without truncation.
- Touch targets are at least 44 × 44 points and remain reachable with one hand.
- Default active play has no body-copy panel. Cards, glass panels and floating
  controls are exceptional. Short language appears in composed darkness or an
  unobstructed native region, never over the material the user is manipulating.

## Rejection tests

A scene fails when any answer is yes:

- Would the experience still work unchanged as a still image with a hotspot?
- Is any action-bearing object or persistent consequence only painted depth
  rather than authoritative 3D geometry and state?
- Could its art, motion and controls be moved to three unrelated chapters?
- Is the clearest light ornamental rather than historical?
- Does the user manipulate a slider, button row or hotspot instead of the
  represented route, resource, institution, pressure or transformation?
- Does a card, load screen or generic transition separate two states that
  should be causally continuous?
- Is 3D being used as decorative camera spectacle rather than as space,
  resistance, time or consequence the user can understand?
- Does reduced motion, high contrast or large text hide the consequence?
- Does the image contain an unresolved material, anatomical or chronological error?
