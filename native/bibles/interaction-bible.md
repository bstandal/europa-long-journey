# Native interaction bible

[`experience-bible.md`](./experience-bible.md) controls chapter-level flow,
human or institutional dependence, causal continuity and proof. This file
controls the domain meaning and input behaviour of interactions inside that
flow.

## The recurring action loop

Every principal interaction follows one state transition:

`orientation → historical action → resistance → response → consequence → world trace`

The user acts on a documented mechanism. Local states may fail, resist or be
reversed, but the approved historical spine remains fixed. There is no avatar,
alternative timeline, correctness score, victory screen or hidden optimum.

## The five grammars

### Trace

Move people, goods, commands, knowledge or force along a real route. Distance,
season, terrain, capacity and infrastructure must visibly affect the action.

### Allocate

Distribute a named scarce resource, obligation, office or status. The result
shows what the distribution sustains and what it leaves exposed. It is not a
resource-optimisation puzzle.

### Assemble

Join the necessary parts of an institution, machine, coalition or legal order.
The result must operate as a system; placing decorative pieces is insufficient.

### Pressure

Apply or hold forces against a frontier, alliance, polity or institution. The
interaction reveals the threshold and the structure that holds or breaks.

### Transform

Move one place, object, population or order through causal stages. A before/
after wipe counts only when the user exposes how the transformation occurred.

## Principal interaction contract

Every `InteractionSpec` declares:

- the named historical mechanism;
- deterministic initial and intermediate states;
- permitted actions and reversible local failure;
- the person, group or institution whose complementary work makes the user's
  contribution necessary;
- immediate visual, sonic and haptic response;
- the declared consequence path: a same-chapter material or systemic test, a
  persistent ending or handoff, or a named later world-trace activation;
- one persistent `WorldEffect`;
- equivalent VoiceOver actions that dispatch the same domain action;
- a fixed Reduce Motion representation of the same result.

An action without a `WorldEffect` is a supporting trace, not a principal
interaction. Inspecting an object, changing prose, opening a pin or scrubbing a
decorative comparison never satisfies the principal contract.

## World effects without player action

History does not wait for input. A named narrative or documentary beat may
prepare, establish or transform a persistent world trace without becoming an
interaction. This is required when input would falsify the mechanism or turn
human catastrophe into play. Such an effect is bound to one concrete source
movement and later to one authored beat; it can never use an unassigned,
synthetic or hash-selected origin. Every principal interaction still produces
its own persistent consequence.

## Pacing

- The opening reaches an unresolved historical pressure before explanatory
  exposition becomes a content page. Each chapter sets and measures its own
  first-action target; there is no generic 90-second allowance.
- An arc contains one to three principal interactions joined by authored
  action, listening, observation and quiet beats that continue the same causal
  movement rather than reset it.
- A principal interaction begins legibly without a tutorial overlay.
- A later principal interaction must change the historical question or
  material meaning. Repeating a mastered gesture under new art is not
  progression.
- The app journals every stable state, so it may be put down at any moment.
- Returning opens at the same causal state; completed actions are not replayed.
- After more than 72 hours, a short optional reorientation may restate the last
  consequence before returning to the exact saved state.

## Guidance hierarchy

The world directs attention before text appears:

1. active resistance or change in the represented mechanism;
2. another person's or institution's attention and complementary action;
3. material sound, spatial relation and historically meaningful light;
4. one short action cue after measured hesitation.

A cue stays beside the acted object, disappears on contact and never becomes a
tutorial overlay. Narration starts only after required precise input releases.

## Input and response budgets

- visible response begins within 50 ms on the supported performance tier;
- active objects remain reachable within a 44-point minimum target;
- drag and hold gestures have a semantically equivalent tap/step action;
- haptics reinforce contact, resistance, transfer, break or seal, and are never
  the sole signal;
- narration pauses at interaction boundaries and never speaks over a required action.

## Prohibited retention devices

No XP, levels, streaks, daily claims, currency, loot, badges, random rewards,
collectible historical figures, quiz gates, countdowns or manipulative push.
Return is earned through the unfinished road, the consequence just created and
the next historical pressure visible in the world.
