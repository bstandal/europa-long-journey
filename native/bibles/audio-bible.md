# Native audio bible

## Franchise voice

The work has one pre-produced English narrator. The voice is controlled,
confident and close enough to carry material detail. It must not use trailer
cadence, theatrical grandeur, generic documentary uplift or mechanical TTS
sentence endings.

Casting is a blocking production gate:

1. Codex produces at least six anonymous candidates with explicit commercial
   rights and zero incremental cost.
2. Every candidate reads the same passage set: physical Neolithic action,
   institutional explanation, conflict, loss, reconstruction, dates and hard
   Greek, Latin, Germanic and Slavic names.
3. The two strongest voices receive uninterrupted twenty-minute stress tests.
4. The user selects one voice in Codex.
5. Model, version, voice identity, settings and pronunciation lexicon freeze.
   If the voice later changes, all narration is regenerated; generations are
   never mixed inside the work.

The canonical narrative script generates reading text, narration cues and
accessibility text. Final alignment must show no added, removed or changed word.

## Performance and files

- Narration is produced in long takes for prosody, then divided at natural
  breaths into stable cues.
- Narration masters: 48 kHz, 24-bit mono before final delivery encoding.
- Score and soundscape masters: 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo.
- True peak is at most −1 dBTP. The final integrated level is calibrated on the
  Chapter 01 slice and held within ±1 LU across chapters.
- Every proper name and foreign phrase enters the versioned pronunciation lexicon.
- A cue fails on a wrong word, pronunciation, stress, breath, metallic break,
  repeated synthetic cadence, click, clipping or audible edit.

## Score

The franchise receives a small motivic and harmonic vocabulary. Each chapter
derives its own material from the chapter’s physical world and mechanism.

- Authoritative music remains editable as symbolic notes, tempo, dynamics,
  articulation and instrumentation before rendering.
- Generated phrases may be raw material; a generated finished track never ships.
- Score is delivered in stems so the runtime can respond deterministically.
- Silence is an authored state, not an absence awaiting background music.
- Generic trailer drums, stock ostinati, undirected pads and identical looping
  four-bar structures are rejected.

## Soundscape and haptics

Every scene may use distinct buses for broad environment, near material work,
the active mechanism, transitions, score and narrator. Every sound has a
visible or historically concrete source. Modern background noise and incorrect
weapons, machines, bells, animals or materials are factual defects.

Haptics use a restrained semantic set: contact, drag, resistance, transfer,
break and seal. The sound and haptic event serve the same historical action.

## Runtime behaviour

- Audio never starts before explicit user choice.
- Narration pauses naturally for required interaction.
- Phone calls, Siri, route changes and backgrounding journal the exact cue and
  sample frame.
- Controlled pause restores sample-exactly; arbitrary hard kill restores within
  250 ms and always preserves exact causal state.
- Cold restoration returns with audio paused.
- Narration, consequences and essential cues remain available offline with the
  installed chapter package.
