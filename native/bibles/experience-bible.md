# Native experience bible

Status: `SHARED_JOURNEY_STANDARD`

This file controls the complete experiential flow of the native Journey across
editorial, interaction, 3D direction, people, audio, pacing and restoration.
Approved chapter contracts and the project constitution remain higher
authority. The interaction, visual, audio and production bibles control their
own disciplines inside this shared experience standard.

## The product test

The Journey is immersive, authored historical play. A chapter fails when it
can be recast unchanged as a sequence of pictures, prose and audio with small
interactive interruptions.

The website and historical manuscript define what the user must understand.
They are production sources for the world, action and narration; they are not a
requirement that the user read the source text inside the app.

## Manus as behaviour score

Every chapter flow describes behaviour before public prose. For each beat it
must identify:

- what pressure, need or unresolved relation already exists in the world;
- what necessary part the user performs;
- which people or institutions prepare, constrain, support, receive or
  continue that action;
- what resistance and immediate material or systemic response occur;
- which stable historical state accepts the action;
- where and when its consequence becomes legible;
- which real carrier leads into the next pressure.

Public language carries historical precision that action, people, sound and
space cannot carry. It cannot substitute for missing behaviour.

## The causal movement

Each chapter has one playable premise and one connected dramatic question. Its
principal movements follow this causal form:

`pressure or need → shared action → resistance → commitment → response → persistent consequence → next pressure`

The result of one movement creates or exposes the next obligation before the
experience can reset. An interaction must change the historical question, not
repeat the same gesture under new art. A resolved task followed by a card,
summary page or unrelated establishment is an episodic break, not a journey.

Action does not mean combat or constant noise. Scarcity, weight, distance,
coordination, sequence, obligation, authority, jurisdiction, information,
institutional dependence and irreversible commitment can all create action.
The pressure must come from the approved history rather than an invented
emergency.

## Bounded and dependent agency

The user is a working presence without an avatar or fictional identity. They
perform one necessary part of real historical work while other people and
institutions remain causally active. The user is never a detached spectator,
solitary hero or omnipotent hand that creates a population, polity, victory or
continental change by itself.

Every principal interaction therefore needs visible or audible dependence:
another person, group or institution must prepare, oppose, support, receive,
complete or continue what the user does. The user can change a local margin,
resilience, order, relationship or incomplete state. The approved historical
outcome remains fixed.

A meaningful action:

- changes weight, tension, volume, access, order, position, authority or
  another state that belongs to the mechanism;
- answers immediately through the acted world;
- can remain safely incomplete and legible without a fail screen;
- leaves a state whose consequence is tested inside the chapter, held as its
  ending or handoff, or named for a later world-trace activation;
- produces its approved persistent `WorldEffect` when complete.

There are no quick-time events, hidden countdowns, correctness scores, game
over screens or fabricated catastrophes added to manufacture intensity.

## Human and institutional stakes

Each chapter chooses a historically plausible human or relational spine. A
recognisable person, household, working group, office, council, unit,
congregation or chain of messengers can carry dependence through the causal
movement. Later periods may repeat a practice, role or gesture without
pretending that one face, object or institution survived unchanged.

Representative people require no invented protagonist, dialogue choice or
biography. They do not look to the user as hint arrows, become meters for
hunger or fear, or wait passively for a heroic rescue. Feeling comes from
shared work, consequence, loss, repair, inheritance, obligation and handoff.

## Continuity across place and time

Every change of place, time or scale follows a real carrier: a person, object,
document, load, route, surface, sound, practice or persistent world trace.
Visible handoffs must establish new people and newly made material when an
interval exceeds a plausible lifetime. No prop, body or voice changes identity
to disguise streaming or a historical cut.

Time advances only after an accepted stable state. The time passage remains
centred on something that survives it, while weather, use, wear, work,
succession or changed authority makes the interval legible.

Observation and silence are valid when they change understanding, expectation
or feeling. They cannot become a resting illustrated page. A natural stopping
place saves the exact causal state without an episode card, confirmation step
or recovery control.

## Meaning before explanation

The world presents the problem before narration names it. Body attention,
material response, spatial relation, sound, light and movement direct the user
before help text appears.

- Narration never competes with precise input.
- Narration names causal meaning or necessary historical precision after the
  user can already read the event.
- Narration does not repeat a visible inventory of objects or rescue an
  uninteresting interval.
- A fallback cue appears only after measured hesitation, stays beside the acted
  object, uses the shortest historical action and disappears on contact.
- Default active play contains no body-copy panel or permanent chapter chrome.

The causal story must pass without narration, captions or score before final
public wording is frozen. Silent comprehension does not require every date or
qualified claim to be visible; it requires the approved situation, mechanism,
dependence, consequence and handoff to be understood from the experience.

## Sensory memory

Sound, haptics and silence form causal state, not decoration.

- Material sound reveals contact, weight, friction, distance, transfer, break,
  absence and return.
- Haptics report the same contact, resistance, transfer, break or seal and
  never act as a reward sting.
- A recurring sensory motif is valid only when a real material, practice,
  institution or state carries it.
- Removing an expected sound can hold deferred consequence; its return must
  arise from the world rather than a musical or interface cue.
- Score never supplies urgency, triumph or scale that staging has failed to
  establish.

A chapter may deliberately use no recurring motif. Repetition is a tool, not a
franchise ornament.

## Pacing and duration

Tempo is calibrated to the chapter's own historical pressure. No Chapter 01
number becomes a universal threshold before measured greybox evidence supports
that promotion.

Every chapter must nevertheless:

- enter an unresolved historical situation before explanatory exposition
  becomes a content page;
- define a timecoded pressure curve and distinguish authored time from
  user-held time;
- keep the next obligation perceptible before relief becomes a reset;
- record hesitation, passive intervals and material-state changes in a
  first-use run;
- rebuild an interval when attention falls before its next historical turn;
- cut an interval when its turn adds no new consequence;
- treat the intended session length as an envelope, never a quota filled by
  waiting, repeated input, slow camera travel or extra prose.

A deliberately quiet chapter can use a lower interaction density. Its silence,
observation and restraint must still change the user's understanding and pass
the same causal-comprehension gate.

## Endings and handoffs

The active world must prove the chapter's final judgement through simultaneous
persistent conditions. A recap interaction, callback montage, glowing map or
narration cannot create a conclusion the state itself does not show.

Control returns while the world remains alive. The last material, route,
institution, pressure or unresolved consequence supplies the concrete handoff
to the next chapter.

## Required chapter experience packet

Before a full greybox starts, every chapter provides:

1. its approved editorial contract;
2. one playable premise and dramatic question;
3. a timecoded flow and pressure curve;
4. an action-to-consequence ledger covering user action, immediate human or
   institutional dependence and the declared consequence path: same-chapter
   test, persistent ending or handoff, or later world-trace activation;
5. a transition and identity map naming the carrier through every place, time
   and scale change;
6. a human or institutional spine;
7. a sensory plan, including deliberate absence where no motif is justified;
8. narration functions and a maximum density, without final wording;
9. natural stop and exact-restoration anchors;
10. explicit chapter-specific pacing hypotheses to measure rather than assume.

Final art, final public wording and full chapter production wait until one
complete greybox passes:

1. a silent run with narration, captions and score disabled;
2. a first-use direct-touch run at natural pace;
3. an uninterrupted run of the complete pressure curve;
4. provisional narration, material sound, silence and haptics;
5. VoiceOver and Reduce Motion through the same causal states;
6. termination and exact restoration during every principal action and
   transition;
7. a complete Codex flow review and byte-bound decision object for the
   editor-in-chief's consolidated approval gate, rather than selected hero
   scenes.

The review records authored time, user-held time, response latency, hesitation,
passive gaps, state changes, failed comprehension and every interval rebuilt or
cut. A beautiful still, isolated interaction or trailer cannot substitute for
this gate.

## Learning transfer

After each full chapter review, classify a finding as:

- `SHARED` — a durable improvement to the Journey floor;
- `CHAPTER_SPECIFIC` — a solution bound to that history, mechanism or
  material;
- `UNPROVEN` — a hypothesis that still needs a complete greybox or device run.

Only `SHARED` findings enter this bible. A shared improvement triggers the
quality ratchet across earlier chapters. Chapter-specific solutions remain in
their flow manuscripts and production briefs. Unproven numbers remain in the
chapter that measures them. Do not maintain a parallel chronological lessons
log.
